\chapter*{The five coupled constructions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{The five coupled constructions}

The governing puzzle is the coexistence of two associative directions on a
factorisable object over a curve.  Ordered fusion acts along a transverse
topological line, whereas associative chiral composition acts through
collision geometry on the curve.  Their products have different carriers and
their bar constructions agree only after a mixed distributive law has been
specified.  The aligned-decomposition idempotent isolates the sector on which
that law is geometrically meaningful.

Five constructions meet along this interface.  Ordered chiral geometry
supplies the two products and their three distinct bars.  Mixed
holomorphic--topological Deligne theory supplies the open--closed comparison.
Igusa--Borcherds theory supplies the root-graded denominator.  Calabi--Yau Hall
geometry supplies extension correspondences and quantum doubles.  The
BV--Einstein construction supplies the deformation and trace carriers.  No
scalar shadow alone determines or constructs these structured objects:
every passage is made by a displayed
bar--cobar, open--closed, Hall, current, trace, or determinant morphism under
its stated hypotheses.

The first construction is developed in detail below.  The remaining parts
record its comparison maps with the other four carriers, retaining separate
completion, parity, descent, and anomaly hypotheses.
