\chapter*{Mathematical scope and claim status}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Mathematical scope and claim status}

Every assertion has a specified source object, target object, construction
map, and hypothesis set. A false identification is replaced by an obstruction
theorem or by the missing comparison morphism. A numerical table counts the
homology of an explicitly displayed chain complex and nothing else.

Statements carry one of five tags. \StatusProved{} is proved under the
displayed hypotheses. \StatusComputed{} is established on an explicitly
stated finite carrier by an exact chain matrix or symbolic identity.
\StatusFormal{} is an identity in a completed or formal category and makes no
analytic-convergence claim. \StatusConditional{} follows from the named
geometric or physical input. \StatusRetracted{} marks a false statement that
is not used as a premise.
