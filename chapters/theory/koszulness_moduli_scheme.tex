\chapter{The Koszulness moduli object}
\label{v1-ch:koszulness-moduli-scheme}

\section{Orientation: from Koszul tests to a moduli object}
\label{v1-sec:kms-orientation}

The fourteen chart tests for chiral Koszulness of a chiral algebra
$\cA$ on a smooth algebraic curve~$X$ with PBW filtration have
four mathematical origins: homological algebra, deformation
theory, factorisation geometry, and tropical geometry. Read at
the Knizhnik--Zamolodchikov associator they appear with unequal
scope. Read as chart tests on the associator torsor, each coordinate
records the scope together with the algebraic datum.

Fix a quadratic chiral twisting datum
$(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i},\kappa_\cA,F_\bullet)$ in the sense of
Definition~\ref{def:koszul-chiral-algebra}.  Its load-bearing map is
the quadratic comparison
\[
 q_\cA\colon \cA^{\mathrm i}\longrightarrow\barBch(\cA),
\]
or, by adjunction,
$p_\cA\colon\Omega^{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA^{\mathrm i})\to\cA$.
The moduli problem below deforms $\kappa_\cA$ and records a
trivialisation of the underlying perfect complex
\[
 C^q_\cA:=\operatorname{Cone}\bigl(\mathsf U(q_\cA)\bigr),
\]
where $\mathsf U$ forgets the coalgebra structure.  Under the chiral
Comparison-Lemma package $H_{\mathrm{CL}}$, this cone is acyclic
exactly when $\kappa_\cA$ is Koszul.  Its framed finite PBW
truncations are open loci cut out by Fitting ideals of the cohomology
of~$C^q_\cA$.  The fourteen named predicates become chart tests after
one supplies comparison quasi-isomorphisms from their finite test
complexes to this quadratic comparison cone.
The word ``moduli'' therefore names four related carriers:
a derived formal functor in Artinian dg directions, a derived
Artin quotient stack before gauge framing, a framed finite-stage
derived affine scheme, and a finite-window test object inside a
fixed PBW truncation. Each carrier retains its stated finiteness and
framing hypotheses.

Section~\ref{v1-sec:kms-moduli} constructs the completed
Koszulness moduli functor
$\cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(\cA)$ and proves its finite-stage
representability under $H_{\mathrm{mod}}$
(Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-moduli}); the construction uses standard
tangent-obstruction theory for Maurer--Cartan functors.
Section~\ref{v1-sec:kms-atlas} assembles the fourteen charts,
each placed at its proposed associator coordinate, and proves the
formal comparison statement: an admissible chart predicate is
equivalent to quadratic chiral Koszulness on the locus where its
comparison complex computes $C^q_\cA$
(Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-fourteen-home-chart}); this identifies
both the coordinate dependence and the comparison hypothesis.
Section~\ref{v1-sec:kms-frontier-closures} formulates the comparison
packages for two frontier items: the representation-theoretic
Virasoro route at the Hodge--Betti chart
(Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-virasoro-noncircular})
and the Yangian chart inclusion
(Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-yangian-embedding}).
Section~\ref{v1-sec:kms-meta} isolates the open meta-Koszulness
problem: a $\mathrm{GRT}_1$-action gives infinitesimal chart
transport, while an $\Etwo$-structure on the tangent category
requires a separately constructed operadic action and its coherence
homotopies.

Three firewalls are fixed. First,
$\cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(\cA)$ is a derived completed carrier;
finite-stage hypotheses produce its algebraic truncations. Second,
the five objects
\[
\cA,\qquad
\barBch(\cA),\qquad
\cA^{\mathrm{i}}=C(s^{-1}V,s^{-2}R),\qquad
\cA^!=(\cA^{\mathrm{i}})^\vee,\qquad
\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)
=\ChirHoch^*(\cA,\cA)
\]
occupy their typed ambient categories.  Here
$\cA=T(V)/(R)$ is the chosen quadratic presentation,
$\cA^{\mathrm i}$ is its quadratic coalgebra, and $\cA^!$ is its
locally finite Verdier dual.  The equivalence
$\Omega^{\mathrm{ch}}\barBch(\cA)\simeq \cA$ is the universal
bar--cobar reconstruction map.  Quadratic Koszul recognition is the
comparison $q_\cA\colon\cA^{\mathrm i}\to\barBch(\cA)$.
The closed-sector object is the chiral Hochschild derived centre, and
physical bulk comparisons factor through that named centre.  Third,
the derived centre is a right-adjoint construction; any higher
operadic structure on the moduli tangent category requires a
separately supplied action.

\begin{remark}[Native versus derived $E_n$]
\label{v1-rem:kms-native-vs-derived-en}
Throughout this chapter, $\cA$ is an $\Eone$-chiral algebra by
hypothesis.  Its derived centre
$\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)=
\ChirHoch^*(\cA,\cA)$ carries the standard higher structure supplied
by the relevant Deligne theorem.  A higher structure on
$\cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(\cA)$ is additional datum.  The proposed
Kontsevich chart~$U_{14}$ concerns an $\SCchtop$ action on the pair
$(\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA),\cA)$ and becomes an
admissible chart after its comparison complex is identified with the
quadratic comparison cone.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Finite-window carriers}

The inputs to the construction are
\begin{itemize}
\item a quadratically presented augmented chiral algebra
 $\cA=T(V)/(R)$ on~$X$ with PBW filtration
 $F_\bullet \cA$ whose associated graded
 $R_\cA = \gr^F\!\cA$ is a Poisson vertex algebra;
\item its quadratic chiral coalgebra
 $\cA^{\mathrm i}=C(s^{-1}V,s^{-2}R)$ and canonical twisting morphism
 $\kappa_\cA\colon\cA^{\mathrm i}\to\cA$;
\item the chiral bar complex
 $\barBch(\cA) = T^c(s^{-1}\bar\cA)$ with deconcatenation
 coproduct, augmentation ideal
 $\bar\cA = \ker(\varepsilon)$, desuspension
 $|s^{-1}v| = |v|-1$;
\item the induced quadratic comparisons
 \[
 q_\cA\colon\cA^{\mathrm i}\longrightarrow\barBch(\cA),
 \qquad
 p_\cA\colon\Omega^{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA^{\mathrm i})
 \longrightarrow\cA,
 \]
 together with the package
 $H_{\mathrm{CL}}(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i},\kappa_\cA)$.
\end{itemize}

The \emph{quadratic Koszul locus} is the locus on which $q_\cA$ is a
quasi-isomorphism, equivalently $p_\cA$ is a quasi-isomorphism, under
$H_{\mathrm{CL}}$.  The universal counit
$\Omega^{\mathrm{ch}}\barBch(\cA)\to\cA$ supplies the ambient
bar--cobar reconstruction on the full augmented domain.

All moduli statements below use the PBW completion. Write
$F_{\le N}\cA^{\mathrm i}$ and $F_{\le N}\barBch(\cA)$ for the
finite PBW truncations and
$\widehat{\barBch}(\cA)=\varprojlim_N F_{\le N}\barBch(\cA)$.
The algebra~$\cA$ is of \emph{finite PBW type} when every
$F_{\le N}\cA^{\mathrm i}$, $F_{\le N}\barBch(\cA)$, and
$F_{\le N}\cA$ is a perfect complex
of $\bQ$-vector spaces with uniformly bounded cohomological
amplitude on compact subsets of the parameter space, and the
transition maps in~$N$ satisfy the Mittag--Leffler condition.
For general PBW towers the object below remains a derived formal
functor.  Finite-type scheme structure enters through the stated
perfectness and stabilization hypotheses.

The finite-stage representability statement uses one further
hypothesis. At PBW stage~$N$ and weight quotient~$m$ of
$\mathrm{GRT}_1$, write
$\mathfrak g_{\Phi,N,m}(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i})$ for the corresponding
finite quotient of
$\mathrm{Conv}_\Phi(\cA^{\mathrm i},\cA)$. The pair
$(\cA,N,m)$ is of \emph{PBW--Artin type} if
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item $\mathfrak g_{\Phi,N,m}(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i})$ is a nilpotent dg Lie
 algebra with finite-dimensional cohomology and bounded
 amplitude;
\item the gauge group
 $\exp(\mathfrak g_{\Phi,N,m}^0(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i}))$ is an algebraic
 unipotent group acting on the finite Maurer--Cartan scheme;
\item the universal finite comparison
 $q_{\Phi,N,m}\colon F_{\le N}\cA^{\mathrm i}\to
 F_{\le N}\barBch_\Phi(\cA)$ varies algebraically and its
 underlying cone $C^q_{\Phi,N,m}$ is perfect with coherent
 cohomology sheaves;
\item the inverse system in~$N$ is derived Mittag--Leffler, so
 ordinary inverse limits compute derived inverse limits.
\end{enumerate}
Under PBW--Artin type the unframed finite-stage moduli is a
derived Artin quotient stack
$[\mathrm{MC}(\mathfrak g_{\Phi,N,m})/\exp(\mathfrak g_{\Phi,N,m}^0)]$
restricted to the open acyclicity locus. After fixing an
associator coordinate and a gauge framing it becomes an open
derived affine scheme. The word \emph{scheme} below always refers
to this framed finite-stage object.

\begin{definition}[Finite-window Koszul test]
\label{v1-def:kms-finite-window-test}
\ClaimStatusDefinitional
\textup{[Type signature: Open quadrant; finite-window quadratic
chiral twisting presentation; Beilinson levels $1\leftrightarrow2$;
hypothesis datum
$(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i},\kappa_\cA,F_\bullet,\Phi,N,m)$.]}
Fix an associator~$\Phi$, a PBW stage~$N$, and a finite weight
quotient~$m$ of $\mathrm{GRT}_1$. A \emph{finite-window Koszul
test} for~$\cA$ is the tuple
\[
\mathsf K_{\Phi,N,m}(\cA)=
\bigl(F_{\le N}\cA^{\mathrm i},F_{\le N}\barBch_\Phi(\cA),
\kappa_{\Phi,N,m},q_{\Phi,N,m},
C^q_{\Phi,N,m},E_r^{\Phi,N,m}\bigr)
\]
with the following typed data.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)},leftmargin=2.4em]
\item $\varepsilon\colon \cA\to\omega_X$ is the fixed
 augmentation and
 $\bar\cA=\ker(\varepsilon)$; the chosen augmentation is part of
 the datum.
\item $F_{\le N}\cA^{\mathrm i}=F_{\le N}C(s^{-1}V,s^{-2}R)$ and
 $F_{\le N}\barBch_\Phi(\cA)$ are conilpotent factorisation
 coalgebras in the chosen $\Phi$-model.  The completed bar object
 $\widehat{\barBch}(\cA)=\varprojlim_NF_{\le N}\barBch(\cA)$ is
 taken in the pro-conilpotent category with continuous coalgebra
 maps.
\item
 $\kappa_{\Phi,N,m}\colon F_{\le N}\cA^{\mathrm i}\to
 F_{\le N}\cA$
 is the finite $\Phi$-coordinate form of the quadratic twisting
 morphism, equivalently a
 Maurer--Cartan element of
 $\mathfrak g_{\Phi,N,m}(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i})$.
\item The induced finite quadratic comparison is
 \[
 q_{\Phi,N,m}\colon F_{\le N}\cA^{\mathrm i}
 \longrightarrow F_{\le N}\barBch_\Phi(\cA).
 \]
 Its test complex is the underlying quadratic comparison cone
 \[
 C^q_{\Phi,N,m}:=
 \operatorname{Cone}\bigl(\mathsf U(q_{\Phi,N,m})\bigr).
 \]
 Under the finite-window restriction of $H_{\mathrm{CL}}$, its
 acyclicity is equivalent to that of the adjoint comparison cone
 $\operatorname{Cone}(\mathsf U(\Omega^{\mathrm{ch}}_\Phi
 (F_{\le N}\cA^{\mathrm i})\to F_{\le N}\cA))$.
\item $E_r^{\Phi,N,m}$ is the spectral sequence induced by the
 PBW and coradical filtrations on~$C^q_{\Phi,N,m}$. It is required
 to be bounded and strongly convergent at the finite window, and
 the inverse system in~$N$ is required to be derived
 Mittag--Leffler before any completed criterion is asserted.
\end{enumerate}
A chart predicate is \emph{admissible} only if it is the
acyclicity of a perfect test complex~$T_{\Phi,N,m}$ equipped
with a filtered quasi-isomorphism
$T_{\Phi,N,m}\xrightarrow{\sim}C^q_{\Phi,N,m}$ compatible with
the transition maps in~$N$ and~$m$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Koszulness moduli object]
\label{v1-def:kms-moduli-scheme}
\ClaimStatusDefinitional
\textup{[Type signature: Open quadrant; derived deformation carrier
of a quadratic chiral twisting datum; Beilinson levels
$1\leftrightarrow2$; datum
$(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i},\kappa_\cA,F_\bullet)$ and its perfect
finite-window quadratic comparison cones.]}
For a complete local Artinian commutative dg
$\bQ$-algebra~$R$ with maximal ideal~$\mathfrak m_R$, set
\[
\widehat{\mathfrak g}_\Phi(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i})
=
\varprojlim_N
\mathrm{Conv}_\Phi
\bigl(F_{\le N}\cA^{\mathrm i},F_{\le N}\cA\bigr),
\]
and twist its differential by the base Maurer--Cartan element
$\kappa_\cA$.  The \emph{Koszulness moduli object} is the derived
functor
\[
\cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(\cA)\colon
\mathbf{Art}^{\wedge}_{\bQ}
\longrightarrow \mathbf{Spaces},
\]
\[
R\longmapsto
\operatorname{hofib}_{0}
\left[
\coprod_{\Phi\in\mathrm{Assoc}(R)}
\mathrm{MC}_{\bullet}\bigl(
\widehat{\mathfrak g}_\Phi(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i})^{\kappa_\cA}
\widehat{\otimes}\mathfrak m_R\bigr)
\xrightarrow{\;\xi\mapsto C^q_{\kappa_\cA+\xi}\;}
\operatorname{Perf}_{\mathrm{ch}}(X;R)^\simeq
\right].
\]
Here the target is the maximal $\infty$-groupoid of perfect
underlying chiral complexes, $0$ is its zero object, and
\[
 C^q_{\kappa_\cA+\xi}
 :=\operatorname{Cone}\bigl(\mathsf U(q_{\kappa_\cA+\xi})\bigr),
 \qquad
 q_{\kappa_\cA+\xi}\colon
 \cA^{\mathrm i}\widehat\otimes R
 \longrightarrow
 \barBch_\Phi(\cA\widehat\otimes R),
\]
is the coalgebra map induced by the deformed quadratic twisting
morphism $\kappa_\cA+\xi$.
Equivalently, an $R$-point may be written
$(\Phi,\xi,h_\xi)$, where
$\Phi\in\mathrm{Assoc}(R)$,
$\xi\in
\mathrm{MC}_{\bullet}(
\widehat{\mathfrak g}_\Phi(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i})^{\kappa_\cA}
\widehat{\otimes}\mathfrak m_R)$, and
$h_\xi\colon C^q_{\kappa_\cA+\xi}\simeq0$ is a
trivialisation in the perfect derived category over~$R$.
An admissible chart coordinate at~$\Phi$ with equivariant transport
is a triple
$(\Pi_\Phi,\mathrm{wit}_\Phi,\gamma_\Phi)$ consisting of
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item a Koszulness predicate $\Pi_\Phi$ depending only on the
 pair $(\cA,\Phi)$ and expressible as acyclicity of a perfect
 test complex $T_{\Phi,N,m}$ at every finite PBW stage and
 finite weight quotient, together
 with a filtered comparison quasi-isomorphism
 $\alpha_{\Phi,N,m}\colon T_{\Phi,N,m}\xrightarrow{\sim}
 C^q_{\Phi,N,m}$;
\item a witness $\mathrm{wit}_\Phi(\cA)$ in the test category
 fixed by~$\Phi$, equivalently a compatible system of
 null-homotopies
 $h_{\Phi,N,m}\colon T_{\Phi,N,m}\simeq 0$ whose images
 under~$\alpha_{\Phi,N,m}$ trivialise the quadratic comparison
 cones;
\item a transition $1$-cocycle of filtered quasi-isomorphisms
 \[
 \gamma_{g,\Phi,N,m}\colon
 C^q_{\Phi,N,m}\xrightarrow{\sim}C^q_{\Phi\cdot g,N,m},
 \qquad g\in\mathrm{GRT}_1(R),
 \]
 compatible with $\alpha_{\Phi,N,m}$ and satisfying the unit and
 composition identities.  Tamarkin formality~\cite{Tamarkin03}
 supplies the operadic source of this transport; the displayed
 comparison with the chiral quadratic cones is part of the chart
 datum.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

Each associator~$\Phi$ fixes a formality coordinate for little
two-disks.  The KZ, Alekseev--Torossian, Hodge--Betti, elliptic, and
Kontsevich constructions furnish five proposed coordinate systems.
Their promotion to charts on the carrier above is the comparison
problem encoded by the maps $\alpha_{\Phi,N,m}$ and
$\gamma_{g,\Phi,N,m}$.  Over a characteristic-zero point,
$\mathrm{Assoc}$ is a torsor under the relevant completed
Grothendieck--Teichm\"uller group.  Finite PBW and weight truncation,
together with PBW--Artin type, produces the framed algebraic
acyclicity locus and its unframed quotient stack.

\begin{theorem}[Koszulness moduli, finite-stage representability]
\label{v1-thm:kms-moduli}
\ClaimStatusConditional
Type signature: \textup{(}Open quadrant, finite-stage
PBW/associator-moduli presentation, Beilinson levels~$1$--$2$,
hypothesis package $H_{\mathrm{mod}}$: a quadratic datum
$(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i},\kappa_\cA,F_\bullet)$ satisfying
$H_{\mathrm{CL}}$, finite PBW type, PBW--Artin finite stages,
algebraic variation of the perfect quadratic comparison
$q_{\Phi,N,m}$, framed associator coordinates, and derived
Mittag--Leffler passage for the completed inverse limit\textup{)}.
Let $\cA$ carry the displayed datum and assume
$H_{\mathrm{mod}}$ at every finite pair~$(N,m)$ used below.
Then $\cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(\cA)$ is a possibly empty derived
formal moduli problem over the Drinfeld associator torsor. For
each finite PBW stage~$N$ and finite weight quotient~$m$ of
$\mathrm{GRT}_1$, the unframed finite-stage truncation is a
derived Artin quotient stack; after fixing an associator
coordinate and a gauge framing, its classical truncation is an
open subscheme of a finite-type affine Maurer--Cartan scheme.
The closed complement is cut out by the Fitting ideals of the
cohomology of $C^q_{\Phi,N,m}$. The completed moduli
object is the derived inverse limit of these finite stages, and
under the Mittag--Leffler hypothesis this is the ordinary
PBW-completed inverse limit.  The affine finite-type carrier is the
framed finite-stage stabilization of this tower.  It has the
following properties.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(A\arabic*)},leftmargin=3em]
\item\label{v1-item:kms-A1}
 For a fixed admissible $\Phi$-model, the base fibre contains
 $(\Phi,0,h)$ precisely when
 \[
 q_{\cA,\Phi}\colon\widehat{\cA^{\mathrm i}}
 \xrightarrow{\sim}\widehat{\barBch}_\Phi(\cA)
 \]
 is a quasi-isomorphism.  Under $H_{\mathrm{CL}}$, this is
 equivalent to
 $p_{\cA,\Phi}\colon\Omega^{\mathrm{ch}}_\Phi
 (\widehat{\cA^{\mathrm i}})\to\cA$ being a quasi-isomorphism,
 and to acyclicity of all $C^q_{\Phi,N,m}$ together with derived
 Mittag--Leffler convergence.  The full-bar counit
 $\Omega^{\mathrm{ch}}\widehat{\barBch}(\cA)\to\cA$ is the
 universal reconstruction map.
\item\label{v1-item:kms-A2}
 At a framed point $x=(\Phi,\xi,h_\xi)$ in a fixed finite-stage
 coordinate, the tangent complex is
 \[
 \mathbb T_x\cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}^{N,m}(\cA)
 \simeq \mathfrak g_{\Phi,N,m}
 (\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i})^{\kappa_\cA+\xi}[1].
 \]
 Allowing the associator coordinate adds
 $\mathbb T_\Phi\mathrm{Assoc}$; passing to the unframed quotient
 takes the homotopy quotient by the infinitesimal gauge action.
 The acyclicity condition is open and therefore adds no
 infinitesimal equation at a point of the locus.  First-order
 deformations and obstructions are the degree~$0$ and degree~$1$
 cohomology of this tangent complex.
\item\label{v1-item:kms-A3}
 Assume in addition the equivariant comparison package
 $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$: filtered transfer equivalences of the
 convolution dg Lie algebras together with the cocycle
 $\gamma_{g,\Phi,N,m}$ of Definition~\ref{v1-def:kms-moduli-scheme}.
 Then $\mathrm{GRT}_1$ transports the chart fibres by equivalences
 preserving $C^q_{\Phi,N,m}$.  A transported component is a
 principal $\mathrm{GRT}_{1,\le m}$-bundle precisely when its
 framed witness fibre is contractible, the finite-dimensional
 quotient action is free, and transport is transitive on that
 component.  The unframed component is the corresponding quotient by
 the automorphism group of the witness.
\item\label{v1-item:kms-A4}
 The fourteen predicates listed below form candidate chart tests:
 $U_1,\ldots,U_{10}$ are centred at $\Phi_{\mathrm{KZ}}$;
 $U_{11}$ at $\Phi_{\mathrm{AT}}$ (Alekseev--Torossian);
 $U_{12}$ at $\Phi_{\mathrm{dR,B}}$ (de Rham--Betti);
 $U_{13}$ at $\Phi_{\mathrm{ell}}$ (Enriquez elliptic); and
 $U_{14}$ at $\Phi_{\mathrm{Kon}}$ (Kontsevich integral).
 A candidate is an admissible derived open chart when a perfect
 complex $T_{j,N,m}$ and a compatible filtered quasi-isomorphism
 $\alpha_{j,N,m}\colon T_{j,N,m}\xrightarrow{\sim}
 C^q_{\Phi_j,N,m}$ are supplied.  Coherent transport between such
 charts uses $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$.
\item\label{v1-item:kms-A5}
 Assume that the shadow brackets vary algebraically over each finite
 Maurer--Cartan scheme and that their ranks are constructible.  Their
 fixed-rank conditions define locally closed substacks.  Thus the
 shadow-class decomposition
 $\mathrm{G} \sqcup \mathrm{L} \sqcup \mathrm{C}
 \sqcup \mathrm{M} \sqcup \mathrm{FF}$ lifts to the
 stratification
 \[
 \cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(\cA)
 = \bigsqcup_{c\,\in\,\{G,L,C,M,FF\}}
 \cM^{(c)}_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(\cA),
 \]
 The proposed computational home coordinates are
 $\Phi_{\mathrm{KZ}}$ on~$\mathrm{G}$
 and on the generic Kac--Moody locus of~$\mathrm{L}$,
 $\Phi_{\mathrm{ell}}$ on the modular locus of~$\mathrm{L}$,
 $\Phi_{\mathrm{AT}}$ on~$\mathrm{C}$, $\Phi_{\mathrm{Kon}}$
 on~$\mathrm{M}$, and $\Phi_{\mathrm{dR,B}}$ on~$\mathrm{FF}$.
 Each becomes an admissible home coordinate through the comparison
 maps required in~\ref{v1-item:kms-A4}; the completed strata are the
 derived Mittag--Leffler inverse limits of the finite strata.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
PBW--Artin type makes the finite Maurer--Cartan functor affine of
finite type and its unipotent gauge quotient a derived Artin stack.
The algebraic universal map $q_{\Phi,N,m}$ is a morphism of perfect
complexes.  Acyclicity of its cone is open: its closed complement is
the union of the supports of the coherent cohomology modules, cut
out by their Fitting ideals.  This proves the finite-stage
representability statement.  Derived Mittag--Leffler identifies the
derived inverse limit with the stated PBW completion.

\emph{\ref{v1-item:kms-A1}.}
The homotopy fibre over zero is nonempty exactly when
$C^q_{\cA,\Phi}$ is acyclic.  The equivalence with the adjoint map
$p_{\cA,\Phi}$ is the fundamental theorem of chiral twisting
morphisms under $H_{\mathrm{CL}}$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:fundamental-twisting-morphisms}), whose quadratic
point-model is Loday--Vallette~\cite[Theorem~3.4.6]{LV12}.
Strong finite-window convergence and derived Mittag--Leffler give
the completed equivalence.

\emph{\ref{v1-item:kms-A2}.}
The tangent complex of a Maurer--Cartan functor at
$\kappa_\cA+\xi$ is the shifted twisted dg Lie algebra.  Restriction
to an open subfunctor preserves this tangent complex.  Variation of
$\Phi$ and gauge descent give the two stated standard modifications.

\emph{\ref{v1-item:kms-A3}.}
The maps in $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$ carry Maurer--Cartan elements and
their induced quadratic comparisons to the corresponding data at
$\Phi\cdot g$.  Their cocycle identities give the action on chart
fibres.  Freeness and contractibility of the witness fibre are
exactly the additional conditions for the stated principal-bundle
conclusion.

\emph{\ref{v1-item:kms-A4}.}
A filtered quasi-isomorphism
$T_{j,N,m}\simeq C^q_{\Phi_j,N,m}$ identifies their acyclicity
loci.  The preceding Fitting-ideal argument makes this an open chart,
and $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$ gives coherence across coordinates.

\emph{\ref{v1-item:kms-A5}.}
Algebraic rank conditions are constructible and their fixed-rank
pieces are locally closed.  The Mittag--Leffler clause assembles the
finite pieces into the completed strata.  Admissibility of each
proposed home coordinate is precisely the comparison requirement
of~\ref{v1-item:kms-A4}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Associator-independence of the Koszulness
property]
\label{v1-cor:kms-grt-invariant}
\ClaimStatusConditional
\textup{[Type signature: Open quadrant; quadratic chiral
associator-transport presentation; Beilinson levels
$1\leftrightarrow2$; hypothesis packages $H_{\mathrm{CL}}$,
$H_{\mathrm{mod}}$, and $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$.]}
Fix a quadratic chiral twisting datum satisfying
$H_{\mathrm{CL}}$ and $H_{\mathrm{mod}}$.  Its intrinsic Koszul
condition is the quasi-isomorphism
$q_\cA\colon\cA^{\mathrm i}\to\barBch(\cA)$.  Assume
$H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$ for the chosen family of formality models.  Then
the equivalences $\gamma_{g,\Phi,N,m}$ identify the quadratic
comparison cones at $\Phi$ and $\Phi\cdot g$; hence the Koszul locus
and its finite Fitting opens are $\mathrm{GRT}_1$-stable.  A named
predicate $\Pi_\Phi$ represents this locus on its home coordinate
when its comparison map
$\alpha_{\Phi,N,m}\colon T_{\Phi,N,m}\xrightarrow{\sim}
C^q_{\Phi,N,m}$ is supplied.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Each $\gamma_{g,\Phi,N,m}$ is a quasi-isomorphism between the two
perfect quadratic comparison cones.  It therefore identifies their
acyclicity loci and their cohomological Fitting ideals.  Compatibility
with $N,m$ and derived Mittag--Leffler passes this identification to
the completion.  The comparison~$\alpha_{\Phi,N,m}$ gives the final
identification with the named predicate.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Kontsevich--Tamarkin, operational form]
\label{v1-rem:kms-kt-operational}
The classical Kontsevich--Tamarkin theorem asserts, over a
field of characteristic zero, that the little two-disks
operad $\mathbf{E}_2$ is formal, with the formality
isomorphism depending on a choice of Drinfeld associator.
Tamarkin formality supplies the operadic change of coordinate.
The package $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$ promotes that change of coordinate to
the convolution models of a fixed quadratic chiral datum and
intertwines their maps to the chiral bar construction.  With this
package, admissible classical characterisations are coordinates over
the associator torsor and their transition maps preserve the
quadratic comparison cone.
\end{remark}

\section{The fourteen charts and their home-coordinate status}
\label{v1-sec:kms-atlas}
% ================================================================

The atlas has fourteen candidate chart tests. Entries $U_1,\ldots,U_{10}$
are the KZ entries at $\Phi_{\mathrm{KZ}}$, taken verbatim from
Chapter~\ref{chap:koszul-pair-structure} with their home
coordinate made explicit. The additional four are stated
here as independent propositions at $\Phi_{\mathrm{AT}}$,
$\Phi_{\mathrm{dR,B}}$, $\Phi_{\mathrm{ell}}$, and
$\Phi_{\mathrm{Kon}}$, each with its own comparison complex. The
mathematical obligation for every entry is an explicit filtered
quasi-isomorphism from its test complex to the finite quadratic
comparison cone at its home coordinate.

\begin{theorem}[Fourteen admissible characterisations on their home chart]
\label{v1-thm:kms-fourteen-home-chart}
\ClaimStatusConditional
Type signature: \textup{(}Open quadrant, finite-stage

\subsection{Virasoro non-circular closure}

An independent Virasoro argument can enter through
representation theory: a strict mixed-Hodge lift of a
Feigin--Fuchs resolution would compute the off-weight complex before
the quadratic Koszul criterion is invoked.  The following statement
records the exact comparison package required for that route.

\begin{theorem}[Virasoro Koszulness, non-circular]
\label{v1-thm:kms-virasoro-noncircular}
\ClaimStatusConditional
\textup{[Type signature: Open quadrant; Hodge--Betti finite-PBW
presentation of the Virasoro vacuum; Beilinson levels
$1\leftrightarrow2$; hypothesis package $H_{\mathrm{Vir,HB}}$:
a strict mixed-Hodge lift of the relevant Feigin--Fuchs resolution,
its off-weight acyclicity, and the comparison
$\alpha^{\mathrm{HB}}_N$ of Proposition
\ref{v1-prop:kms-hodge-betti-chart}.]}
For the Virasoro vertex algebra $\Vir_c$ at generic central
charge $c \notin \{c_{p,q} : (p,q) \in \bZ^2_{>0},
\gcd(p,q)=1\}$, assume $H_{\mathrm{Vir,HB}}$.  Then the bar
cohomology is supported on the quadratic diagonal; equivalently,
\[
 H^\bullet\!\left(T^{\mathrm{HB}}_N(\Vir_c)\right)=0
 \qquad\text{for every finite }N.
\]
Consequently $q_{\Vir_c}\colon\Vir_c^{\mathrm i}\to
\barBch(\Vir_c)$ is a quasi-isomorphism after PBW completion, so
$\Vir_c$ is quadratically chirally Koszul.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The package $H_{\mathrm{Vir,HB}}$ makes the strict
mixed-Hodge resolution a model for $T^{\mathrm{HB}}_N(\Vir_c)$ and
supplies its off-weight acyclicity.  The comparison
$\alpha^{\mathrm{HB}}_N$ identifies this complex with
$C^q_{\Phi_{\mathrm{dR,B}},N}$.  Proposition
\ref{v1-prop:kms-hodge-betti-chart} and derived
Mittag--Leffler identify the completed quadratic comparison cone as
acyclic.  Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-moduli}\ref{v1-item:kms-A1}
then gives the asserted quasi-isomorphism $q_{\Vir_c}$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Why this closes the classical circularity]
\label{v1-rem:kms-fm197-closed}
The logical route is representation-theoretic:
Feigin--Fuchs/Kac data $\to$ strict mixed-Hodge resolution
$\to T^{\mathrm{HB}}_N\to C^q_N\to q_{\Vir_c}$.  Its unresolved
step is the construction of the strict mixed-Hodge lift and of
$\alpha^{\mathrm{HB}}_N$ for the Virasoro vacuum family.  A direct
construction of those maps closes the Virasoro branch, with
$q_{\Vir_c}$ as the Koszul detector and the universal bar--cobar
counit as the ambient reconstruction map.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Yangian chart inclusion}

The associative Koszulness of the Yangian $Y_\hbar(\fg)$
for~$\fg$ simple uses the Polishchuk--Positselski nonhomogeneous
Koszul framework \cite{Positselski11}. The chiral question is
whether this Koszulness upgrades to the chiral setting. The
moduli reformulation phrases this as a chart inclusion.

\begin{theorem}[Yangian chart inclusion]
\label{v1-thm:kms-yangian-embedding}
\ClaimStatusConditional
\textup{[Type signature: Open quadrant; associative-to-chiral
finite-PBW comparison presentation; Beilinson levels
$1\leftrightarrow2$; hypothesis package: a finite-PBW chiral avatar,
filtered comparison of quadratic cones, representability,
Shapovalov-locus reflection, $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$ compatibility, and
derived Mittag--Leffler completion.]}
For the Yangian $Y_\hbar(\fg)$ with~$\fg$ simple, assume that the
evaluation-module construction gives a finite-PBW $\Eone$-chiral
avatar, carries the associative quadratic twisting datum to the
chiral datum by filtered quasi-isomorphisms of the finite quadratic
comparison cones, and defines a representable chart functor that
reflects the Shapovalov open locus.  Assume also that the functor
intertwines the two $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$ cocycles. Then the framed
finite PBW associative Koszulness moduli embeds as a
$\mathrm{GRT}_1$-equivariant open subfunctor of the framed
finite-stage chiral Koszulness moduli:
\[
\cM^{\mathrm{assoc}}_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(Y_\hbar)
\;\hookrightarrow\;
\cM^{\mathrm{chiral}}_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(Y_\hbar).
\]
At a fixed finite PBW stage and gauge framing this map is an
open immersion of affine schemes. In the completed unframed
setting it is the induced morphism of derived quotient stacks,
provided the PBW tower is Mittag--Leffler. Chart-surjectivity remains
open: it asks whether every chiral chart lifts a classical
associative chart.  A higher-rank chiral atlas would resolve this
remaining comparison problem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For $\Phi = \Phi_{\mathrm{KZ}}$, the associative PBW chart
$U_1^{\mathrm{assoc}}$ detects $Y_\hbar(\fg)$-Koszulness in
the classical Polishchuk--Positselski sense
\cite{Positselski11}, framed as a filtered-CDG-Koszul
deformation of $U(\fg[t])$ with level-filtration-compatible
nonhomogeneous relations. For all simple Lie algebras
(classical types via Molev's PBW \cite{Molev2007}; exceptional
types $E_6, E_7, E_8, F_4, G_2$ via Guay--Regelskis--Wendlandt
\cite{GRW18}) this chart is nonempty.

Transport the associative chart coordinate to the chiral
setting through the assumed finite-PBW $\Eone$-chiral avatar
(Definition~\ref{def:e1-chiral-yangian},
Chapter~\ref{ch:ordered-associative-chiral-kd}). The RTT data
then defines the formal-disk chiral structure through the
evaluation modules~$V_u$, and the PBW filtration is compatible with the
chiral bracket. The associative chart coordinate
$(\Phi_{\mathrm{KZ}}, c^{\mathrm{assoc}}_{\mathrm{KZ}})$ maps
to the chiral chart coordinate
$(\Phi_{\mathrm{KZ}}, c^{\mathrm{chiral}}_{\mathrm{KZ}})$:
the assumed comparison identifies their finite quadratic cones.
Hence
$\cM^{\mathrm{assoc}}_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(Y_\hbar)
\hookrightarrow
\cM^{\mathrm{chiral}}_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(Y_\hbar)$
is defined on framed finite PBW stages.  Compatibility with the two
$H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$ cocycles makes it $\mathrm{GRT}_1$-equivariant.

By the open-locus hypothesis, the associative chart
$U_1^{\mathrm{assoc}}$ is the complement of the zero locus of the
Shapovalov determinant, and its preimage in
$\cM^{\mathrm{chiral}}_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(Y_\hbar)$ is the
complement of the same zero locus, hence open. Passing to the
completed unframed object is legitimate only under the
Mittag--Leffler and PBW--Artin hypotheses stated in
Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-moduli}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Exceptional-type Yangian PBW input via GRW18]
\label{v1-cor:kms-exceptional-PBW}
\ClaimStatusProvedElsewhere
Via Guay--Regelskis--Wendlandt \cite{GRW18}, the Yangians
$Y_\hbar(E_6)$, $Y_\hbar(E_7)$, $Y_\hbar(E_8)$,
$Y_\hbar(F_4)$, $Y_\hbar(G_2)$ admit PBW bases, closing the
exceptional-type PBW input for the finite-stage comparison.  The
remaining inputs are an exceptional finite RTT/FRT packet, the
finite-PBW chiral avatar, and the open-locus comparison hypotheses.
\end{corollary}

\begin{remark}[Associative versus chiral atlas for Yangians]
\label{v1-rem:kms-fm161-closed}
The comparison between associative and chiral Koszulness atlases for
Yangians is additional structure. The moduli reformulation makes its
scope precise at framed finite PBW stage: the associative atlas
is an open subfunctor of the chiral atlas. The open question is
surjectivity: every lifted chart belongs to the associative image,
while genuinely chiral charts would form its complementary locus.
For $\fg = \mathfrak{sl}_2$ the embedding is an isomorphism and the
associative PBW data captures the full chiral Koszul datum
(Theorem~\ref{thm:sl2-yangian-triangle-concrete});
for higher rank, chart-surjectivity is open and is the
correct form of the associative--chiral comparison question.
\end{remark}

\label{v1-sec:kms-meta}
% ================================================================

Under $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$, the Koszulness carrier has a
$\mathrm{GRT}_1$-action on framed admissible charts by
\ref{v1-item:kms-A3}.  Its differential gives an action of
$\mathfrak{grt}_1$ by derivations on the tangent category.  An
$\Etwo$-structure requires operations in every arity together with
their chain-level coherence; the following problem isolates that
additional construction.

\begin{problem}[Meta-Koszulness]
\label{v1-thm:kms-meta-koszulness}
\ClaimStatusOpen
\textup{[Type signature: Open quadrant; finite framed tangent-category
presentation; Beilinson levels $1\leftrightarrow2$; input packages
$H_{\mathrm{mod}}$, $H_{\mathrm{chart}}$, and
$H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$; required new datum: a chain-level
$C_*(E_2)$-action compatible with quadratic-cone comparisons.]}
Fix a finite PBW--Artin stage~$(N,m)$ and a gauge framing. The
tangent dg category of admissible coordinates in
$\cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}^{N,m}(\cA)$ already carries the unary
derivations induced by $\mathfrak{grt}_{1,\le m}$.  Construct an
operad morphism
\[
 C_*(E_2)\longrightarrow
 \operatorname{End}\bigl(\mathbb T
 \cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}^{N,m}(\cA)\bigr)
\]
whose unary infinitesimal symmetries recover this action, whose
higher operations commute with
$\alpha_{j,N,m}$ and $\gamma_{g,\Phi,N,m}$, and whose finite-stage
operations assemble under derived Mittag--Leffler completion.
Such a construction would give a pro-$\Etwo$ tangent category; the
appropriate higher Deligne theorem would then give an $E_3$-structure
on its Hochschild derived centre.  Willwacher's identification
$H^0(GC_2)\cong\mathfrak{grt}_1$~\cite{Willwacher15} supplies the
degree-zero graph-complex symmetry.  The required operad morphism is
the remaining chain-level obligation.

This target is the derived-centre specialization of
Convention~\ref{conv:master-five-object-firewall} and
Theorem~\ref{thm:typed-verdier-koszul-firewall}.  The sought
$E_3$-object is the Hochschild derived centre of the tangent
category; the chiral algebra, its bar coalgebra, its quadratic
coalgebra, and its Verdier--Koszul dual retain the ambient categories
specified by that canonical firewall.
\end{problem}

\begin{remark}[Meta-Koszulness and the chart count]
\label{v1-rem:kms-fm198-closed}
The KZ-coordinate count overstates the generic-parameter
standard landscape. The honest count there is seven
intrinsic bidirectional equivalences plus a
Barr--Beck--Lurie restatement, a Theorem~H-conditional chiral
Hochschild consequence, a perfectness-conditional Lagrangian criterion, and
a one-directional $\mathcal{D}$-module purity implication.  The
carrier separates the formal chart implication from the open
higher-structure problem: each classical condition becomes a chart
after its quadratic-cone comparison is supplied, and
Problem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-meta-koszulness} asks for the operadic action
on the tangent category. Counting by proposed home associator produces the
10~KZ $+$ 1~AT $+$ 1~dR,B $+$ 1~ell $+$ 1~Kon $=$ 14 chart
decomposition of
Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-fourteen-home-chart}.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Functor of points]
\label{v1-rem:kms-construction-not-narration}
The moduli object $\cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(\cA)$ is constructed as
the derived functor of points
$(\Phi,\xi,h_\xi)$ in Definition~\ref{v1-def:kms-moduli-scheme}.
Here $\xi$ deforms $\kappa_\cA$ and $h_\xi$ trivialises

$H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$.
\end{remark}

\section{Consequences of the Koszulness moduli object}
\label{v1-sec:kms-summary}
% ================================================================

The Koszulness moduli object is the derived deformation carrier of
the quadratic twisting datum.  Its points are associator coordinates,
deformations of $\kappa_\cA$, and trivialisations of the quadratic
comparison cone.  Its consequences inherit the packages stated
above.

First, the seven-core-plus-satellites split of the
KZ presentation (seven intrinsic bidirectional equivalences;
Barr--Beck--Lurie as a listed consequence; conditional
factorization homology; Theorem~H-conditional chiral Hochschild consequence;
conditional Lagrangian criterion; one-directional
$\mathcal{D}$-module purity) resolves as a KZ-coordinate restriction:
under $H_{\mathrm{chart}}$, each of the fourteen
characterisations is equivalent on its home coordinate to quadratic
chiral Koszulness
(Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-fourteen-home-chart}).

Second, under $H_{\mathrm{Vir,HB}}$, the Hodge--Betti comparison
turns the strict Feigin--Fuchs mixed-Hodge resolution into a
representation-theoretic proof of quadratic Virasoro Koszulness
(Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-virasoro-noncircular}).

Third, under the comparison hypotheses of
Theorem~\ref{v1-thm:kms-yangian-embedding}, the associative
Koszulness atlas of the Yangian
$Y_\hbar(\fg)$ embeds as an open subfunctor of the framed
finite-stage chiral atlas;
Guay--Regelskis--Wendlandt \cite{GRW18}
discharges exceptional-type PBW.

Under $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$, the Kontsevich--Tamarkin change of
formality coordinate acts on the quadratic comparison cones and
transports admissible charts.  Problem
\ref{v1-thm:kms-meta-koszulness} asks for the further
$C_*(E_2)$-action on the completed tangent category.  The fourteen
entries record the selected classical test list over the
pro-unipotent associator torsor.

\begin{remark}[Reading the atlas as the Koszul Reflection orbit]
\label{v1-rem:kms-koszul-reflection-bridge}
\index{Koszul reflection!atlas as orbit of K-squared identity}
Under $H_{\mathrm{chart}}$ and $H_{\mathrm{GRT}}$, the fourteen
admissible characterisations of

comparison locus through fourteen associator-coordinate windows. The

$\mathrm{G}\sqcup\mathrm{L}\sqcup\mathrm{C}\sqcup\mathrm{M}\sqcup\mathrm{FF}$
of property \ref{v1-item:kms-A5} is the partition of $\Kosz(X)$
by computational complexity of the bracket on the chiral derived
centre
$\cZ^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)=\ChirHoch^*(\cA,\cA)$;
the Climax (Chapter~\ref{chap:climax-platonic}) packages the

universal ghost functor (Chapter~\ref{chap:universal-conductor}).
The Koszulness moduli object thereby organises the twelve listed
conditions of
Theorem~\textup{\ref{thm:koszul-equivalences-meta}} (Chapter~\textup{\ref{chap:koszul-pairs}})
into seven intrinsic bidirectional comparisons and five consequences,
conditional criteria, or one-directional tests attached to
specific comparison hypotheses. The atlas preserves these logical
statuses while recording their home-coordinate data.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Siegel-modular Koszul pair moduli]
\label{rem:kms-1}
The K3 base point has a Siegel modular comparison chart: the
Pasol--Zagier 2013 universal Igusa form $\Phi_{10}$ on
$\mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb Z)\backslash\mathbb H_2$ records the
denominator identity
$\prod(1-q^{r\alpha})^{c(r\alpha)}=\Phi_{10}$ for the
bar object $\barBch(\mathbf H_{\Delta_5})$ and the Verdier-dual
object $(\mathbf H_{\Delta_5})^!$. This Siegel quotient is a finite
Hodge-theoretic comparison locus on the K3 component.
Definition~\ref{v1-def:kms-moduli-scheme} supplies the derived
carrier $\cM_{\mathrm{Kosz}}(\mathbf H_{\Delta_5})$; the
Humbert divisor $H_1\cup H_4$ is the discriminant of that
comparison, and
$[\Phi_{10}]\in H^2(\mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb Z)\backslash\mathbb H_2)$
is its Siegel modular class
\cite{PasolZagier2013,GritsenkoNikulin1998}.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Moduli stratification by Humbert type]
\label{rem:kms-2}
The Siegel comparison locus stratifies by Humbert type. The
$H_1$-stratum, where a genus-$2$ curve splits as a product of
elliptic curves, hosts the bi-based Ran restriction to
$\mathrm{Ran}(E^{\mathrm{nod,sm}}_{24})$. The $H_4$-stratum
hosts the Kuga--Satake image of K3 surfaces with Picard rank
$\geq 17$ (Shioda--Mitani). The open complement hosts the
generic K3 Hodge structure. The monodromy of
$\mathcal L^{\Delta_5}$ around $H_1\cup H_4$ is the
Mukai--Humbert face of the $\mathbb Z/8$-class
$K^{\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}}$ \cite{Bruinier2002,KugaSatake1967};
its scope is the Hodge-theoretic K3 comparison chart.
\end{remark}
