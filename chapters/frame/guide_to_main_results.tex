\noindent
The principal results below are stated with their labels, locations,
ambient categories, hypotheses, and epistemic status.  Theorem~A is
the universal reconstruction theorem in the enhanced associative Ran
ambient.  Theorem~B is the quadratic recognition theorem for a chosen
presentation.  Factorization restriction, completed chain
realization, Verdier transport, and family geometry enter through
their named hypothesis packages for augmented chiral algebras~$\cA$
on a smooth projective curve~$X$
(\S\ref{sec:standing-assumptions}).

\subsection*{The ordered bar and averaging}
\label{subsec:guide-ordered-bar-record}

The local seed of the volume is the logarithmic normal form
\(\eta_{ij}\) along a collision diagonal, represented by
\(d\log(z_i-z_j)\) only on affine/formal genus-zero screens. Arnold's
three-term relation supplies the affine/formal configuration-space part of
nilpotence for the ordered bar coalgebra
\[
 \barB^{\mathrm{ord}}_X(\cA)=T^c(s^{-1}\bar\cA),
 \qquad |s^{-1}v|=|v|-1,
\]
while the chiral multiplication, internal differential, and curvature
terms supply the algebraic part of the bar differential. In the ordered
model the universal Maurer--Cartan element is
\[
 \Theta_\cA^{\mathrm{ord}}\in \MC(\mathfrak g_{\cA}^{\Eone}),
 \qquad
 D\Theta_\cA^{\mathrm{ord}}
 +\tfrac12[\Theta_\cA^{\mathrm{ord}},\Theta_\cA^{\mathrm{ord}}]=0.
\]
Its degree-two local component is the collision residue
$r_\cA(z)$; its scalar projection is the modular characteristic
$\kappa(\cA)$.  The fixed-fibre curved identity is
\[
 \operatorname{tr}_{\mathrm{diag}}\!\bigl(m_0^{(g)}\bigr)
 =\kappa(\cA)\cdot\omega_g,
\]
and the raw chain identity is $m_1^2=[m_0,-]$.  For $g\geq2$,
Theorem~D begins with
$\operatorname{Obs}^{\mathrm{def}}_g(\cA)\in H^2(\Def_g(\cA))$.
Under~$H_D^1$, the pointed class
$\operatorname{Obs}^{\mathrm{def}}_{1,1}(\cA)$ has trace
$\kappa(\cA)\lambda_1$.  Under~$H_D^K$, the virtual class is
$\mathfrak O_g^K(\cA)=\kappa(\cA)\lambda_{-1}(\mathbb E_g)$ and
\[
 \operatorname{ch}_g\!\left(\mathfrak O_g^K(\cA)\right)
 =(-1)^g\kappa(\cA)\lambda_g,
 \qquad \lambda_g=c_g(\mathbb E_g).
\]
The comparison with the deformation class is $H_D^{\mathrm{tr}}$.
Under~$H_D^{\mathrm{graph}}$, multi-weight algebras carry the
numerical cross-channel term of
Theorem~\ref{thm:multi-weight-genus-expansion}.

The symmetric modular class is the derived quotient of the ordered
datum under the universal conductor. In the braided case this means
the $L_R$-twisted completed coinvariant descent
\[
 \barB^\Sigma_{X,n}(\cA)
 \;\simeq\;
 \bigl(\barB^{\mathrm{ord}}_{X,n}(\cA)\otimes L_R\bigr)
 ^{\mathbb L,\wedge}_{\Sigma_n},
\]
under the hypothesis package consisting of QYBE for~$R$, strong
unitarity $R^{21}(-z)R^{12}(z)=\id$, regular-singular
Fulton--MacPherson extension of~$L_R$, compatibility with collision
products, and conilpotent completion. In the untwisted
characteristic-zero splitting this descent is represented by the
Reynolds formula
\[
 \mathrm{av}_n=\frac1{n!}\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\sigma^*.
\]
For $\Einf$-chiral input the symmetric and ordered models agree on the
formal commutative surface. For genuinely $\Eone$-chiral input the
$\mathrm{GRT}_1(\mathbb Q)$-dependent ordered conductor is erased by
the descent; the quotient therefore has a nontrivial kernel. The Vol~I finite
standard-landscape atlas has quadrichotomy
$\mathsf G/\mathsf L/\mathsf C/\mathsf M$ with shadow depths
$2/3/4/\infty$. Extended comparison rows adjoin
\[
\mathsf M^{\mathrm{ext}}\quad\text{and}\quad\mathsf B,
\]
where $\mathsf B$ is the conjectural Vol~III Mukai/K3 comparison row,
adjoined to the four Vol~I archetypes. The represented Vol~I witnesses are
Heisenberg, affine Kac--Moody at
non-critical level, $\beta\gamma$, Virasoro with principal
$\mathcal W_N$ extensions, subregular or hook Drinfeld--Sokolov
reductions such as Bershadsky--Polyakov.  The conjectural comparison
row is represented by the K3 Borcherds--Kac--Moody object
$\mathbf H_{\Delta_5}$. The critical affine stratum is the boundary
where the Sugawara denominator vanishes.

\noindent\textbf{Five derived evaluations.}\enspace
Theorem~A starts in the pro-nilpotent monoidal category
\[
 \mathcal C_X=\bigl(D(\operatorname{Ran}X),\otimes^{\mathrm{ch}}\bigr).
\]
Francis--Gaitsgory's enhanced bar--cobar equivalence for the reduced
associative operad, followed by re-unitalization, gives the universal
equivalences
\[
 \Omega_XB_X(\cA)\xrightarrow{\sim}\cA,
 \qquad
 \cC\xrightarrow{\sim}B_X\Omega_X(\cC)
\]
for augmented Ran algebras and conilpotent Ran coalgebras.  The
factorization restriction carries status \(\ClaimStatusConjectured\)
under (H_{\mathrm{fact}}); the completed chain realization carries
status \(\ClaimStatusConditional\) under (H_{\mathrm{conv}}).
Under (H_{\mathrm{VD}}), Verdier duality constructs the algebra
\[
 K_X(\cA):=\mathbb D_{\operatorname{Ran}}B_X(\cA)
\]
and its biduality comparison.

Theorem~B begins with a connected positive-weight quadratic
presentation
\[
 \cA=T_X(V)/(R),
 \qquad
 \cA^{\mathrm i}=C_X(s^{-1}V,s^{-2}R),
\]
and the canonical coalgebra morphism
\[
 q_\cA\colon\cA^{\mathrm i}\longrightarrow B_X(\cA).
\]
Under (H_{\mathrm{CL}}(\cA,\cA^{\mathrm i},\tau_{\mathrm i})), the
following conditions are equivalent: (q_\cA) is a
quasi-isomorphism; (Omega_X(\cA^{\mathrm i})\to\cA) is a
quasi-isomorphism; the two quadratic twisted tensor products are
acyclic; and the PBW/bar spectral sequence has diagonal support and
strong convergence.  A family-level Koszul theorem therefore amounts
to a calculation of
\(H^*\operatorname{Cone}(q_\cA)\) together with the convergence
package.  Positselski's fixed-coalgebra co/contra correspondence gives
a separate comparison between module categories attached to a chosen
curved coalgebra.

Theorem~C is complementarity for the ordinary centre local system.
C0 identifies the degree-zero cohomology sheaf of the strict flat fibre
with~$\mathcal Z(\cA)$.  C1 applies the represented Verdier involution to
\[
 \mathbf C_g(\cA)
 =R\Gamma(\overline{\mathcal M}_g,\mathcal Z(\cA))
\]
and gives its eigenspace decomposition; perfectness and the
nondegenerate anti-invariant pairing give the Lagrangian polarization
for $g\ge1$, with genus~$0$ treated separately.  Theorem~H concerns the
brace dg algebra
$Z^{\mathrm{der}}_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)=\ChirHoch^\bullet(\cA,\cA)$.
A supplied comparison~$\iota_Z^{\mathrm{der}}$, followed by~$H^0$ and
C0, connects the two theorem surfaces.  C2 is the shifted-symplectic/BV
realization on the BV-perfect, Verdier-nondegenerate,
uniform-weight-perfect bar-chart surface. On the
canonical landmark witnesses, the computed Vol.~I values are
\[
 \kappa(\cA)+\kappa(\cA^!)
 \in\{0,13,250/3\}.
\]
The value $8=2c_+(\widetilde H(K3,\mathbb Z))$ is a
\ClaimStatusConjectured{} $\mathsf B$-row candidate.  Its proved input
is the Mukai-lattice signature $(4,20)$; its chiral interpretation
requires the chart, Koszul-duality, scalar, modular, and quantum
comparison hypotheses $(H_{\mathrm{chart}},H_{\mathrm{KD}},
H_{\mathrm{scalar}},H_{\mathrm{mod}},H_{\mathrm{quant}})$.  The
Bershadsky--Polyakov central-charge identity is
$c(k)+c(-k-6)=50$.  Its modular characteristic is
\ClaimStatusOpen{} pending the genus-one minimal-DS curvature
calculation; the proposed normalization $\kappa=c/6$ would adjoin the
conditional value~$25/3$.  The scalar in this sentence is
$K^\kappa=\kappa(\cA)+\kappa(\cA^!)$.  The central-charge sum and the
Borcherds weight $\operatorname{wt}(\Delta_5)=5$ are separately typed
invariants.

Theorem~D is conditional on the relevant comparison package.  Under
$H_D^1$, the pointed class
$\operatorname{Obs}^{\mathrm{def}}_{1,1}(\cA)$ has trace
$\kappa(\cA)\lambda_1$.  Under~$H_D^K$, the higher-genus scalar
virtual object is
$\kappa(\cA)\lambda_{-1}(\mathbb E_g)$ and its top Chern-character
component is $(-1)^g\kappa(\cA)\lambda_g$.  Under
$H_D^{\mathrm{graph}}$, the numerical genus expansion is
\[
 F_g(\cA)=F_g^{\mathrm{sc}}(\cA)
 +\delta F_g^{\mathrm{cross}}(\cA),
 \qquad
 F_g^{\mathrm{sc}}(\cA)=\kappa(\cA)\lambda_g^{\mathrm{FP}}.
\]
$\delta F_1^{\mathrm{cross}}=0$, and the scalar-diagonal graph package
gives $\delta F_g^{\mathrm{cross}}=0$. For $\mathcal W_3$ at genus~$2$ the
cross-channel correction is
\[
 \delta F_2^{\mathrm{cross}}(\mathcal W_3)=\frac{c+204}{16c}.
\]

Theorem~H is the curve-level chiral Hochschild support theorem.  A
family datum $H_H(\cA;S)$ gives a complete chart model $Q_\cA$, a
quasi-isomorphism
$Q_\cA\xrightarrow{\sim}R\!\operatorname{Hom}_{\cA^e}(\cA,\cA)$,
and a strong deformation retract onto $K_{\cA,S}$, whose terms are
supported in~$S$.  Consequently
\[
 \operatorname{Supp}\ChirHoch^\bullet(\cA)\subseteq S,
 \qquad
 P_\cA(t,q)=\sum_{n\in S}\dim_qH^n(K_{\cA,S})t^n
\]
when these graded pieces are finite.  The bounded Virasoro benchmark
has support $\{0,2,3\}$; the rank-one even superboson benchmark has
dimensions $(2,1,0,\ldots)$.  A bounded-to-chart quasi-isomorphism is
the hypothesis which transports either calculation to the curve
chart.  A perfect degree-$d$ pairing is a second theorem surface and
gives the reflection $n\leftrightarrow d-n$.
The Calabi--Yau higher-degree shadows belong to the Vol.~III
companion lane.  One proposed degree-three K3$\times E$ target is
\[
 \chi_3={:}T\partial T{:}-\tfrac14\partial^3T
 +\hbar\,\mathrm{qt}(J^{(3)}),
\]
Its proposed pairing with the K3$\times E$ generator is
\[
 \langle[\chi_3],[e_3^{\,\mathrm{K3}\times E}]\rangle
 =2\,\mathrm{Vol}(E)(2\pi i)^3
 =\chi(\mathcal O_{\mathrm{K3}})
 \Res_{s=1/2}[E_5^{(2)}(Z,s)]\big|_{K(1)}.
\]
This pairing has status \ClaimStatusConjectured{} under the named
Vol.~III comparison package.  These classes refine the cross-volume
picture, while Theorem~H retains the curve-level statement above.

The bar--cobar, quadratic, fixed-coalgebra, and Hochschild constructions
have distinct outputs.  Theorem~C studies
$R\Gamma(\overline{\mathcal M}_g,\mathcal Z(\cA))$ after the strict-flat
C0 comparison, Theorem~H studies the derived chiral centre, and
Theorem~D supplies the normalized trace from C1 to the scalar lane.

\noindent\textbf{The collision residue.}\enspace

tiers. Tier~I records intrinsic OPE data and the
shifted-symplectic/BV datum on its perfect locus:
\[
 r^{\mathrm{Heis}}_k(z)=\frac{k}{z},
 \qquad
 r^{\mathrm{KM},\fg}_k(z)=\frac{k\,\Omega_{\mathrm{tr}}}{z},
 \qquad
 r^{\mathrm{Vir}}_c(z)=\frac{c/2}{z^3}+\frac{2T}{z}.

Tier~II is the $E_d$-holomorphic factorisation-algebra braiding,
pinned up to the $\mathrm{GRT}_1(\mathbb Q)$ torsor by

Stage-2 chiral quantum-group output: Maulik--Okounkov spectral
$R$-matrix on $\mathrm{Hilb}^{[n]}(\mathrm{K3})$, Drinfeld RTT
current via the associative bar multiplication, and Belavin elliptic
non-dynamical $R$-matrix. Cross-tier maps specialise from Tier~I to
Tier~II to Tier~III; intra-tier maps are
$\mathrm{GRT}_1(\mathbb Q)$-gauge equivalences.

\noindent\textbf{Three-volume comparison.}\enspace
On their common theorem surfaces the three volumes form the diagram
\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
\mathrm{CY}_d^{\mathrm{cat}}
& \xrightarrow{\;\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d\;}
& \mathrm{Fact}^{\mathrm{hol}}_{E_d}(X) \\[2pt]
\big\downarrow\,{\scriptstyle\mathrm{Shad}_\bullet}
& & \big\downarrow\,{\scriptstyle
\mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}} \\[2pt]
\mathrm{Alg}_{\Eone^{\mathrm{ch}}}(C)
& \xleftarrow[\;\Omega_C^{\mathrm{ch}}\;]{\barB_C^{\mathrm{ch}}}
& \mathrm{CoAlg}^{\mathrm{fact}}_{\Eone^{\mathrm{ch}}}(C).
\end{array}
\]
Volume~I is the bottom-row operadic reading. Volume~II is the
holographic reading: $\mathsf{SC}^{\mathrm{ch,top}}$ governs the
derived-centre pair $(C^\bullet_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA,\cA),\cA)$, and
topologisation produces the $3$d holomorphic-topological bulk where
the DVV--DMVV inverse Igusa form $1/\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}$
appears on its BPS scope.
Volume~III is the geometric reading: the two-stage functor
\[
 \Phi_d^{(\Sigma_{d-1},C)}=
 \mathrm{Sp}^{\mathrm{ch}}_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\Phi_d^{\mathrm{FA}}
\]
first produces an $E_d$-holomorphic factorisation algebra and then
specialises by factorisation homology over $\Sigma_{d-1}$ followed by
restriction to a reference curve~$C$. A single Calabi--Yau category
therefore gives a family of chiral shadows indexed by
$(\Sigma_{d-1},C)$.

On the K3 lane the Stage-2 shadow has local positive-half Rees charts
where Hall multiplication and hCS extension gluing agree. The compact
Hall--Drinfeld/Borcherds object is the conditional global comparison
\[
 \mathbf H_{\Delta_5}
 =\mathcal D_\hbar\!\left(
 \mathcal Y^{\mathrm{Hall}}_\hbar(\mathrm{CoHA}_{\mathrm{K3}\times E}),
 \widetilde\Phi_{\mathrm{Sp}_4}^{\mathrm{Sieg\text{-}Bor}}
 [\Phi_{10}^{\mathrm{un}}/\eta^{24}],
 R_{\mathrm{Sieg,dyn}}\right),
 \qquad \hbar^2=-\frac18.
\]
Vol~III constructs the resolved-conifold hCS \(E_3\)-descent, its
anomaly-free perturbative quantum lift, and the finite positive-half
hCS--Rees-Hall chart maps. The global display also assumes the
quasi-NCCR and compact Hall-double identifications.
Four Yangian types
$Y_\hbar(\mathfrak g)$, $Y_\hbar^{\mathrm{dg}}(\mathfrak g)$,
$Y^{\mathrm{ch}}(\mathfrak g)$, and $Y^{\mathrm{spec}}$ separate the
Drinfeld, dg-shifted, chiral, and spectral outputs. Four
$\Psi$-siblings
$\{\Psi,\Psi^{\deg},\Psi^{\mathrm{tor}},\Psi^{\mathrm{metap}}\}$
record the reflective interior, Klingen cusp, Humbert divisor, and
metaplectic branch of the Stage-2 output.
The Borcherds weight identity is
\[
 \kappa_{\mathrm{BKM}}(\Phi_N)=\frac{c_N(0)}2.
\]
The Gritsenko family has
$c_N(0)=(10,6,4,3,2)$ and weights $(5,3,2,\tfrac32,1)$ on its
five-rung index set \textup{(}Jatkar--Sen,
Govindarajan--Krishna; the $N=4$ member carries the half-integral

set. The physical CHL family has weights
$24/(N+1)-2=(10,6,4,2,1,0)$ on

$(5,2,1,1,\tfrac12,1,\tfrac14,0)$ at $N\in\{1,\ldots,8\}$, with cover
assignment
$\mathrm{Sp}_4(\mathbb Z)/\mathrm{Mp}_4/\widetilde{\mathrm{Mp}}_4$ by

unambiguous only after the input Jacobi form is named.

The proposed trace normalization on a lattice row is
\[
 \hbar^2K=-1,\qquad K=2c_+(L).
\]
For the Mukai lattice $\mathrm{II}_{4,20}$, lattice arithmetic gives
$2c_+=8$ and hence the candidate normalization $\hbar^2=-1/8$.
Its status is \ClaimStatusConjectured{} under
$(H_{\mathrm{chart}},H_{\mathrm{KD}},H_{\mathrm{scalar}},
H_{\mathrm{mod}},H_{\mathrm{quant}})$.  Bruinier's
\cite[Lemma~5.1]{Bruinier2002} concerns cyclotomic coefficient fields
for vector-valued cusp forms; the integer~$8$ occurs there in
$N'=\operatorname{lcm}(N,8)$ in half-integral weight.  Lusztig's
root-of-unity construction takes the root order as input.  A future
$H_{\mathrm{mod}}$ theorem must construct the Humbert comparison, and
a future $H_{\mathrm{quant}}$ theorem must select order~$8$ from the
Mukai datum.  The identity component is
$\mathrm{Aut}^\circ(\mathrm{K3}\times E)=E$.  A two-parameter
K-theoretic CoHA deformation therefore requires an additional torus
action or a derived replacement; this requirement belongs to
$H_{\mathrm{quant}}$.

\subsection*{Modular-completion comparison}

MC1 through MC4 form the finite theorem core. MC5 has proved analytic
and strict Mittag--Leffler pro-object / $J$-adic /
filtered weight-completed chain surfaces, plus conditional genus-$0$
BRST/bar and all-genera coderived comparison surfaces. Analytic HS-sewing
holds at all genera (Theorem~\ref{thm:general-hs-sewing}); at genus~$0$
the algebraic BRST/bar comparison is conditional at
Theorem~\ref{thm:algebraic-string-dictionary}; BV=bar in the coderived
category is conditional on the harmonic-factorization package
