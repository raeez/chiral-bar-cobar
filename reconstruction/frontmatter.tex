This volume is the normative reconstruction of four manuscript witnesses and the extended repository corpus.  It is neither a concatenation nor a claim that every sentence in the archive was correct.  The governing rule is stronger:
\begin{quote}
Every retained assertion has a specified source object, target object, construction map, and hypothesis set.  Every discarded identification is replaced by a no-go theorem or by the missing comparison morphism.  Numerical tables count only the homology of an explicitly displayed chain complex.
\end{quote}
The 153-page witness supplied the cleanest exact locality law through the aligned-decomposition projector.  The 246-page witness preserved the indispensable audit of the legacy Motzkin and Riordan tables.  The earlier 198- and 199-page witnesses preserved the obstruction statements that later global rewrites had erased.  The repository supplied calculations, higher-genus kernels, modules, Yangian material, Hall products, trace brackets, and automorphic products.  These sources are reconciled here by theorem, not by chronology.

\begin{statusbox}[title={Claim-status convention}]

\section*{The central correction}
The phrase ``noncommutative chiral algebra'' had been used for two different structures.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A \emph{transversely ordered chiral algebra} is an \(\Eone\)-algebra for the pointwise coefficient tensor product in factorisable \(D\)-modules.  It models a theory on \(X\times\mathbb R\): collision occurs on \(X\), while the associative word is ordered along \(\mathbb R\).
\item An \emph{associative chiral algebra} is an \(\Eone\)-algebra for chiral convolution, or equivalently an algebra over a non-symmetric associative chiral operad.  On the formal disc its field realization is a nonlocal vertex algebra; an exchange operator satisfying unitarity, the spectral Yang--Baxter equation, and braided locality upgrades it to a quantum vertex algebra in the sense initiated by Etingof--Kazhdan.
\end{enumerate}
Neither structure determines the other.  The object demanded by genuinely doubly noncommutative examples carries both, together with an explicit mixed distributive law.  The aligned-decomposition idempotent records exactly the sector in which that mixed law is geometrically defined.

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
Let \(X\) be a smooth complex algebraic curve and let \(\FactD(X)\) denote a stable presentable category of factorisable right \(D\)-modules.  The Ran formalism carries two products.  The componentwise coefficient tensor \(\tensorpt\) preserves a fixed finite support, while chiral convolution \(\starc\) assembles disjoint supports.  Their finite-set combinatorics is controlled by Hadamard and Cauchy products of species.  There is no global interchange isomorphism.  Instead there are canonical inclusion and projection maps whose composite is the idempotent onto the common-decomposition sector.  Sheaf-level locality occupies precisely this sector; strong duoidality occurs only on aligned windows.

A transversely ordered chiral algebra is
\[
 A\in \Alg^{\aug}_{\Eone}(\FactD(X),\tensorpt),
\]
and its interval bar is
\[
 \Barop_X^{\perp}(A)=\one\tensor_A^{\mathbb L}\one
 \simeq \int_{([0,1],\partial[0,1])}A.
\]
Its differential multiplies adjacent transverse letters and does not extract collision residues.  An associative chiral algebra is instead
\[
 C\in \Alg^{\aug}_{\Eone}(\DMod(\Ran X),\starc),
\]
or an algebra over the corresponding associative chiral operad.  Its operadic bar contracts chiral composition trees and retains the entire diagonal distribution.  A doubly associative chiral algebra consists of both structures and a coherent mixed law.  The two bars may then be iterated or totalized; without that law they are unrelated constructions with similar notation.

The homotopy-coherent generators-and-relations theory is obtained from a Boardman--Vogt or bar--cobar resolution of the two-coloured operad.  Transverse edges, chiral edges, and their product orientation produce the Stasheff equations, chiral associativity equations, and mixed interchange equations.  Derived presentations are homotopy pushouts of free algebras, not ordinary quotients.  On nilcomplete augmented algebras and conilcomplete coalgebras, internal bar and cobar are inverse.  In positive weight the equivalence has a direct finite-window proof.  The augmentation boundary, double centralizer, two-sided twisting transform, and derived centre form the corrected Koszul--Morita square.  Quivers require relative bars over their vertex-idempotent algebra; matrix and Weyl algebras require relative, Rees, or curved replacements because ordinary augmentations do not exist.

The volume calculates a ladder of increasingly noncommutative examples.  It gives complete bar complexes for free and square-zero algebras, the quantum and Jordan planes, relative quadratic quivers, enveloping algebras, and an augmented noncommutative Frobenius algebra.  It proves that Zamolodchikov--Faddeev normal ordering is confluent exactly when the exchange matrix satisfies Yang--Baxter and unitarity; develops the RTT Yangian with its Hopf and PBW structures; derives both \(A_2\) Hall--Serre relations by finite-field flag counts; and constructs the necklace Hamiltonian action and stable trace Poisson bracket
\[
 \{p_{ab},p_{cd}\}=(ad-bc)p_{a+c-1,b+d-1}.
\]
The legacy Motzkin and Riordan formulas are retained as planar generating functions and explicitly rejected as universal bar cohomology.  The correct ordered bars of \(U(\mathfrak{sl}_2)\), \(U(\mathfrak{sl}_3)\), and \(U(\mathfrak g_2)\) are their Chevalley--Eilenberg homologies.

A second transverse direction produces exchange; it is not contained in \(\Eone\) order.  KZ transport, RTT exchange, and four-dimensional Chern--Simons theory are compared only after their monodromy and fibre-functor hypotheses are stated.  A BRST derivation acts factorwise on the ordered bar and gives a convergent comparison spectral sequence under explicit completeness and Kunneth assumptions.  Cyclic transverse sewing and Deligne--Mumford chiral sewing have independent genus variables.  Their compatible graph transform satisfies
\[
 d\Theta+\frac12\brX{\Theta}{\Theta}+\frac12\brp{\Theta}{\Theta}
 +\hbarX\DeltaX\Theta+\hbarp\Deltap\Theta=0.
\]
A concrete tensor-product sewing model realizes the mixed recognition theorem and gives an exact bivariate genus amplitude.  Determinant curvature, BV anomaly, BRST anomaly, framing anomaly, and curved Koszul curvature remain classes in different deformation complexes until a named map relates them.

Finally, for a positively root-graded Lie superalgebra, the Euler supercharacter of the ordered bar is the denominator product
\[
 \chi_\Gamma\Barop^{\perp}U(\mathfrak n_+)
 =\prod_{\alpha>0}(1-e^{-\alpha})^{\sdim\mathfrak g_\alpha}.
\]
The Igusa product is recovered as a typed Borcherds denominator identity.  A compact Hall realization remains a separate geometric theorem rather than a consequence of matching scalar products.
