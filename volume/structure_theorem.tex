\chapter{The structure theorem}
\label{ch:structure}

\section{The complete datum}

\begin{definition}[Complete ordered chiral datum]
\label{def:complete-datum}
A complete datum is a tuple $\bigl(\mathcal F, m_\perp, m_X, \lambda,
\varepsilon, \{\text{completions}\}\bigr)$ in which $\mathcal F$ is a
factorisation coefficient on $X$; $m_\perp$ is an augmented transverse
$E_1$ multiplication for $\otimes^!$; $m_X$ is an augmented associative
chiral $E_1$ multiplication for $\starch$; $\lambda$ is an aligned mixed
distributive law with all higher homotopies; $\varepsilon$ is a scalar or
relative augmentation appropriate to the algebra; and the nilpotent,
weight, pole, coradical, and analytic completions are each specified.
\end{definition}

Any subset of the tuple defines the corresponding partial theory. No
missing component is inferred from the others.

\begin{maintheorem}[Structure theorem]
\label{thm:structure}
Let $A$ be an augmented transversely ordered chiral algebra on a smooth
complex algebraic curve $X$. On the complete subcategories specified in
the preceding chapters, the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Linear species carry the aligned-decomposition inclusion,
  projection, and idempotent; for factorisation $D$-modules, locality is
  the family of disjointness-locus squares.
  \hfill{\small Theorems \ref{thm:aligned-splitting}, \ref{thm:locality}}
\item Strong duoidality holds exactly on aligned windows, and a mixed
  distributive law can be written only on the common-decomposition sector.
  \hfill{\small Corollary \ref{cor:aligned-duoidal},
  Proposition \ref{prop:lambda-sector}}
\item The ordered bar is a simplicial object of $\FactD(X)$ whose
  realization is the interval amplitude $\unit\otimes^L_A\unit$, and it is
  a conilpotent coassociative coalgebra in factorisation coefficients.
  \hfill{\small Proposition \ref{prop:bar-factorises},
  Theorem \ref{thm:interval}}
\item Bar and cobar are inverse on the nilcomplete and conilcomplete
  locus, with an independent elementary proof in connected positive weight.
  \hfill{\small Theorems \ref{thm:koszul-internal},
  \ref{thm:positive-reconstruction}}
\item The augmentation-boundary algebra is $A^!=\RHom_A(\unit,\unit)$, and
  the double centralizer is the derived augmentation completion.
  \hfill{\small Definition \ref{def:koszul-dual},
  Theorem \ref{thm:double-centralizer}}
\item Quadratic, nonhomogeneous PBW, and curved duality are comparison
  constructions to the universal bar resolution, not definitions of $A^!$.
  \hfill{\small Theorems \ref{thm:quadratic}, \ref{thm:curved-pbw}}
\item The two-sided Koszul transform carries the diagonal bimodule to the
  diagonal bicomodule, relative tensor to derived cotensor, and Hochschild
  to coHochschild theory; $\End(\id_{\mathrm{Mod}_A})\simeq
  \RHom_{A^e}(A,A)$ carries the universal internal $E_2$ structure.
  \hfill{\small Theorem \ref{thm:koszul-morita}}
\item The bar of an enveloping algebra is Chevalley--Eilenberg chains under
  $K$-flatness, positive grading, completeness, finite decomposition, and
  internal PBW.
  \hfill{\small Theorem \ref{thm:bar-chevalley}}
\item For a positively root-graded Lie superalgebra the Euler
  supercharacter of the ordered bar is the denominator product, and the
  Igusa product is recovered as a typed Borcherds denominator identity.
  \hfill{\small Theorem \ref{thm:bar-denominator},
  Corollary \ref{cor:igusa}}
\item For the positive Witt algebra the bar homology is two dimensional in
  every positive degree, in the pentagonal weights, and the Euler
  character is $\prod_{n\ge1}(1-q^n)$.
  \hfill{\small Theorem \ref{thm:pentagonal-rigidity}}
\item Exchange, trace, and modular sewing are functorial extensions with
  independent sources; the ribbon and chiral genera are independent
  variables.
  \hfill{\small Chapter \ref{ch:two-directions}}
\item Every numerical invariant is the homology, trace, period, monodromy,
  index, or Euler character of a previously constructed object.
  \hfill{\small Chapter \ref{ch:enveloping-audit}}
\item The obstruction constitution prevents residues, topology, central
  charge, scalar sequences, boundary centres, and anomalies of different
  types from being promoted into structures they do not determine.
  \hfill{\small Chapter \ref{ch:no-go}}
\end{enumerate}
\end{maintheorem}

\begin{proof}
Each item is the cited statement of the preceding chapters. No item is used
in the proof of another except as displayed there.
\end{proof}

\section{Hierarchy of scalarisation}

The object-level theory precedes its scalar traces. A functor may pass
through
\[
  \FactD(X) \longrightarrow A \longrightarrow \Barperp(A)
  \longrightarrow Z(A) \longrightarrow \mathrm{Rep}(H)
  \longrightarrow K_0 \longrightarrow \text{character},
\]
and a central charge, a Hall polynomial, a knot invariant, a determinant,
or an automorphic form occurs at a specified position. An equality of
scalars is explained by a comparison morphism between the structured
objects that precede those scalars, and by nothing else.

\section{What remains open}

Three constructions are named and absent, and no theorem of this volume
supplies them.

\begin{enumerate}
\item An explicit mixed distributive law $\lambda$ for any example, with
  verification that it lands in the common-decomposition sector. Until
  then Definition~\ref{def:doubly} has no instance and the doubly
  noncommutative theory is a definition without a model.
  \hfill{\small Remark \ref{rem:lambda-open}}
\item The anticommutation of the two edge-contraction differentials in the
  two-coloured cofibrant resolution, via independence of the two
  determinant lines. The transverse and chiral edge differentials must
  satisfy $d_\perp d_X + d_X d_\perp = 0$ for the mixed
  codimension-two boundary equation to close; the orientation argument is
  asserted in the witnesses and is not proved here.
\item The genus-two-and-above analytic material, prime forms, plumbing
  coordinates, degeneration, and Siegel theta, re-sorted into the
  independent $g_\perp$ and $g_X$ variables. It exists at volume in the
  archived corpus under a single fused genus and has not been ported.
  \hfill{\small \texttt{PORT\_LEDGER.md}}
\end{enumerate}
