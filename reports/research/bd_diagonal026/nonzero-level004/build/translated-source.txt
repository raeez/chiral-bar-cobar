Translated ordered states and native Heisenberg collisions




                                              Abstract

    Differentiating the ordered multiplication kernel determines the products of translated states.
These products define a flat differential module and retain the original ordered bar as its untranslated
subcomplex. At a fixed nonzero Heisenberg level, weighted edge integrals give binary and ternary
graded comparisons with the native chiral complex. Their defects determine minimal added collision
modules for these prescribed maps. The ternary addition contains three pair-diagonal modules and
a current module on the small diagonal. Exact sequences describe its cohomology relative to the
original ordered source. A four-current calculation determines the next differential, supported on
the three double-pair diagonals.
