Four-input ordered coefficients and native Heisenberg collisions




                                                Abstract

       The two disjoint adjacent collisions of four ordered inputs define a square of coefficient forms.
   Weighted integration on this square supplies the double-pair component of a native Heisenberg
   comparison. Its boundary identity determines the remaining defects on the triple, double-pair, and
   small diagonals. An explicit complex of translated current modules absorbs these defects at the orig-
   inal nonzero level. The construction gives a permutation-equivariant cochain map for every global
   four-input coefficient. The cohomology of the added complex and the resulting relative cohomology
   are described by Weyl-module presentations. Ordered three-plus-one cuts agree with the ternary
   comparison on their factorization domains. A torsion calculation obstructs the corresponding strict
   two-plus-two extension.

                                       𝐵2 ( 𝑍 −2 ( 𝑃2 )) = [ 𝐽 | 𝐽 ] · 𝑡 𝐷2 .
The separated translated-current module is free cyclic over 𝐷2 , because 𝑇 𝑟 𝐽 ⊠ 𝑇 𝑠 𝐽 are independent.
The right ideal 𝑡 𝐷2 is proper: the nonzero simple delta is a quotient generator killed by 𝑡 . Hence the
pure separated current [ 𝐽 | 𝐽 ] does not belong to this cycle image.
   Preserving the comparison on 𝑤12 requires precisely that pure current as the coefficient of 𝜈12 . The
cycle condition just proved makes this impossible. Cross localization does not invert 𝑡34 and preserves
the nonzero delta quotient on its diagonal. Thus the same contradiction holds there.
