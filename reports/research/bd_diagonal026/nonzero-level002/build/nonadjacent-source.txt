     The nonadjacent collision and its source extension




                                              Abstract

     At nonzero Heisenberg level, the first-to-third collision cannot be represented by outputs sup-
ported on the adjacent diagonals. Its minimal cyclic source module over regular position functions
has length two in the normal direction. Adjoining this module supplies the missing source differen-
tial on the open complement of the adjacent diagonals. The resulting comparison takes values in the
unextended chiral algebra. An exact sequence computes the change in source cohomology globally.
It includes a length-four quotient on the small diagonal and an explicit failure of quasi-isomorphism.
An all-word variant uses the same added module and has a length-three small-diagonal quotient.
The new collision module has a specified right differential module presentation. A separate binary
calculation excludes a regular coefficient-compatible connection on the unchanged ordered symbols.
