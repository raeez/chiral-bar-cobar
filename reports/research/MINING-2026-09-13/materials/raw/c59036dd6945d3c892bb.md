The following shared definitions are part of every applicable record.

For a finite ordered prime set of size \(d\), a commutative ring \(R\), and units \(q_{ij}\in R^\times\), let
\[
A_q=R\langle e_1,\ldots,e_d\rangle/
(e_i^2,\ e_je_i+q_{ij}^{-1}e_ie_j),\qquad |e_i|=1,\quad d_A=0.
\]
Squares are imposed even in characteristic two. Let
\[
C_q=\bigoplus_{\nu\in\mathbb N^d}Rc_\nu,\qquad |c_\nu|=0,
\]
with coproduct coefficient
\[
Q(a,b)=\prod_{i<j}q_{ij}^{a_jb_i},
\quad
\Delta c_\nu=\sum_{a+b=\nu}Q(a,b)c_a\otimes c_b.
\]
The twisting cochain sends \(c_{\epsilon_i}\mapsto e_i\), and all other basis elements to zero. The small cup model is
\[
Z_q=\operatorname{Hom}_R(C_q,A_q)
=A_q\otimes_R R_q[[x_1,\ldots,x_d]],
\quad x_jx_i=q_{ij}x_ix_j,
\quad d=[\delta,-],\quad\delta=\sum_i e_ix_i.
\]
The formal factor means a coefficientwise product over occupations. No Hilbert norm or bounded contraction is implicit.
