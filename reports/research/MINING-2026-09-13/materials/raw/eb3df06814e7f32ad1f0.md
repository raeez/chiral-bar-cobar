1. **C01 — Finite tensor-nilpotent reconstruction and its Ran application.** Context §§10.1–10.4, `[134292,141437)`, especially Theorems 10.1–10.2.

   The actual setting is a stable enhanced **nonunital** linear monoidal category with exact tensor and a finite bound annihilating sufficiently long tensor products. The bar has suspended words, adjacent multiplication differential and coassociative deconcatenation; the cobar has desuspended words and coproduct differential. It is the associative operad throughout.

   Reconstructing \(\Omega BA\) by the number of original \(A\)-letters gives, in the associated graded, one two-state complex at every gap. Its differential is an isomorphism between the “inside a bar word” and “between cobar factors” states, with the suspension sign. The tensor of these gap complexes contracts whenever a gap exists. Only the one-letter term survives. For \(B\Omega C\), the coproduct increases original-letter number, so the finite **descending** filtration is required. This proves the actual counit and unit equivalences when the bar–cobar adjunction and its coherent construction are supplied.

   The first deciding example is the two-cardinality algebra
   \[
   A=M\oplus(M\otimes_{\mathrm{ch}}M),
   \]

The inclusion of the primitive \(M[1]\) into the bar is a coalgebra quasi-isomorphism.
