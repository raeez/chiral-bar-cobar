**Reconstructed Volume I claims**

All positive conclusions below use explicit coefficient hypotheses rather than silently treating “characteristic zero” as “field.”

- **E01 — what observables forget.** The preface’s distinction between multiplication, fibre multiplication, and higher operations has concrete witnesses. T’s family \(A_\lambda=k\{1,x,\zeta\}\), \(x^2=\lambda x+\zeta\), \(x\zeta=\zeta^2=0\), has constant commutator Lie algebra and Frobenius pairing but varying multiplication. The doubled-line calculation below distinguishes fibre algebras with equal dimensions. These are distinct observation functors; none alone establishes a reconstruction theorem.

- **E02 — word bar–cobar reconstruction.** Over a field, for an augmented dg algebra with positive integral weights, the weight-\(w\) bar–cobar complex has at most \(w\) letters. Filtering by letter count leaves tensor products of the contractible two-state gap complex for words of length at least two. Empty and one-letter terms survive. The finite filtration proves the counit quasi-isomorphism. Source: T §2.1, pp.7–8; E preface and chapter-2 navigation. This establishes the positive-weight associative result, not a chiral coefficient comparison, unaugmented reduced bar, or arbitrary completed equivalence. Those extensions remain separate dependencies.

- **E03 — doubled-line coefficient nonformality.** Source: E §9.1, physical p.46. For \(R=k[z]\), \(K=R[z^{-1}]\), the bounded flat dg algebra
  \[
  C=[R\oplus R\xrightarrow{\,b-a\,}K]
  \]
  has componentwise degree-zero multiplication, left action \((a,b)v=av\), right action \(v(c,d)=vd\), and square-zero degree-one product. Its unit is \((1,1)\). Its cohomology is \(R\oplus(K/R)[-1]\). Derived specialization gives \(k\times k\) for \(C\), but \(k[\theta]/\theta^2\) for the cohomology algebra, using the flat resolution \([R\to K]\) of \(K/R\). Their idempotents/nilpotents differ. This independently proves relative dg-algebra nonformality. It does not prove that all other coefficient models are nonformal.

- **E04 — principal parts and transgression.** Source: E §§9.2–9.6, physical pp.47–48. \(K/R\otimes_R^{\mathbf L}k\simeq k[1]\), whereas its ordinary tensor is zero. The residue functional is not \(R\)-linear to \(R/(z)\): \(\operatorname{Res}(z\,z^{-2}dz)=1\). Restriction \(C\to R\) kills degree-one classes; recovering a boundary operation therefore needs the displayed shifted kernel/transgression and a state-space action. Polynomial integration gives a contraction over a \(\mathbf Q\)-algebra, but is not multiplicative for the Alexander–Whitney cup product.

- **E05 — binary orders and cyclic branches.** Source: E §§10.1–10.2, physical pp.50–51. For \(R_I=k[z_i]\), order charts glue on localizations at reversed-pair differences. At a residue point with equality blocks \(B\), the fibre has \(\prod_B |B|!\) reduced points. Independent pair orientations instead give \(2^{\sum_B\binom{|B|}{2}}\). A tournament with no directed triangle is transitive, so removing cyclic equality loci recovers the total-order chart scheme. For three coincident labels, the counts are eight versus six. The later local-cohomology correction complex was not independently reconstructed here; its exactness remains with the chapter-10 owner.

- **E06 — the relative diagonal centre.** Source: E chapter 13, physical pp.65–68. Let \(X=X_I\), \(R=R_I\), \(C=R\Gamma(X,\mathcal O_X)\), retaining its coherent commutative structure. Finite affine intersections give compactness of \(\mathcal O_X\). Residue-field detection and the finite discrete fibres give generation. Consequently, for \(D=C\otimes_R^{\mathbf L}C\),
  \[
  C\otimes_D^{\mathbf L}C\simeq C,\qquad
  C\xrightarrow{\sim}R\operatorname{Hom}_D(C,C)=Z_R(C).
  \]
  The homological epimorphism is **\(D\to C\)**; the argument does not assert \(R\to C\) is one. For an associative \(C\)-algebra \(A\), fully faithful monoidal restriction of \(C\)-linear bimodules gives \(Z_R(A)\simeq Z_C(A)\). This is an internal associative \(E_2\) statement.
