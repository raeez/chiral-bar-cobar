- **C01 — Mixed chart gluing; §33.1, Proposition 33.2, physical 147–148. Survives.** For disjoint finite sets \(P,Q\), take \(R_{P,Q}=k[z_i,w_a,(w_a-w_b)^{-1}]\), one chart per total order of \(P\), and invert precisely the bulk differences whose order varies on an intersection. This is algebraic geometry, with no analytic half-space comparison supplied.

- **C02 — Bounded associative Čech model; §33.2, Theorem 33.3. Survives.** The finite direct sum over increasing chart-index lists, with alternating restrictions and Alexander–Whitney multiplication, is a bounded dg associative algebra of flat modules over the coordinate ring. Affine intersections and the finite Čech resolution compute derived global sections. Dependencies: C01 and the explicit differential/product in Chapter 31, additionally read.
