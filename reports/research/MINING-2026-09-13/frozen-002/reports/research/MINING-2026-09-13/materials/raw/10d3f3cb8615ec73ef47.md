**§§23.1–23.2 — Order configurations and disjoint-cluster factorization.**

\[
E(\sigma,\rho)\subseteq E(\sigma,\tau)\cup E(\tau,\rho),
\]
so the coordinate-identity gluing maps satisfy the cocycle condition. For a partition \(\mathcal P\), restriction of orders identifies the resulting scheme, over the locus of distinct cross-block coordinates, with the product of the block schemes. Interleavings become identified there; restriction through refinements is strict.

Independent deciding case: three labels have one generic branch, two branches when exactly two labels collide, and six branches at total collision.
