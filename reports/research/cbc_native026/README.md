The new mathematical source has the following proved constructions and explicit obstructions, subject to fresh independent acceptance.

1. **Polynomial classical DS reduction.** The differential graded algebra is `C[e,h,f] tensor Lambda(b,c)`, with degrees `0,0,0,-1,1`. Its differential satisfies `Db=e-1`, `Dh=-2ec`, `Df=hc`, and `De=Dc=0`. The actual chain algebra maps are `i(u)=ef+h^2/4` and `p(e,h,f,b,c)=(1,0,u,0,0)`. Their composite `pi` is the identity. The two-stage Koszul/Chevalley computation gives cohomology `C[u]` in degree zero. Tensorwise maps give quasi-isomorphisms of the direct-sum associative reduced bars. 2. **A genuine limit of the polynomial comparison.** The projection fails Poisson compatibility: `p{h,f}=-2u`, while `{p(h),p(f)}=0`. It therefore does not construct a Poisson, BV, vertex, or chiral comparison. No specialization or completion bridge is asserted.
3. **Second-differential transport.** An actual equivariant columnwise quasi-isomorphism of bounded double complexes yields the explicit total chain map. Both anticommutation and the finite totalization are stated and used. Two separate odd square-zero operators can have anticommutator one. See `ds_chain_comparisons.tex:131`.
4. **The generic Virasoro target obstruction.** For `c(k)=1-6(k+1)^2/(k+2)`, the full conformal ghost charge on the universal vacuum is nilpotent exactly at `k=-8/3,-7/2`.
