\begin{abstract}
The principal \(\mathcal W_3\) algebra provides the first two-generator
test of the Open Beilinson tower.  Its local structure is exact: the
Fateev--Lukyanov central charge, the reflected central sum, the complete
Zamolodchikov \(WW\) product, the norm and zero mode of the composite
\(\Lambda\), and the level-one Shapovalov curve.  The presentation
coalgebra, DS/bar transport, modular trace, stable-graph sewing, scalar
shadows, Hamiltonians, and physical open--closed lift are named
reconstruction problems.  The chapter separates this exact local
algebra from every comparison theorem required to assemble the
seven-entry datum.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Reader's map}

The \emph{holographic modular Koszul datum} is the seven-entry package
\[
\bigl(
\mathcal A,\mathcal A^{\mathrm i},\mathcal A^!,
Z_{\mathrm{ch}}^{\mathrm{der}}(\mathcal A),
K_{\mathcal A}^{\mathrm{coll}},
\Theta_{\mathcal A},\nabla^{\mathrm{hol}}
\bigr).
\]
A chosen presentation supplies
\(\mathcal A=T_X(V)/(R)\),
$\cA^{\mathrm i}=C_X(s^{-1}V,s^{-2}R)$, and
$q_\cA\colon\cA^{\mathrm i}\to\barB_X^{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)$.
The completed bar supplies
$K_X(\cA)=\mathbb D_{\Ran}\barB_X^{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA)$.
The physical interpretation is formed under the line comparison and
open--closed comparison packages; the line category is attached to the
strict Koszul entry, while the closed-sector entry is the derived
centre.  The exact local algebra constrains these constructions, and
the named reconstruction packages build them.
