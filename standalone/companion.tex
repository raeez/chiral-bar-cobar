\begin{abstract}
\noindent\itshape
Classical Koszul duality is the theory of the bar construction over a
point. Volume I rebuilds it over a smooth complex curve and then over
the moduli of curves: the bar differential becomes an integral
transform on Fulton--MacPherson compactifications with kernel
$\eta=d\log(z_1-z_2)$, its square vanishes exactly by the Arnold
relation together with the Borcherds identity, and at genus $\ge1$
the construction acquires curvature
$d^2_{\mathrm{fib}}=\kappa(\cA)\,\omega_g$ whose scalar $\kappa$ is
the central charge read as modular curvature. All structure assembles
into one Maurer--Cartan element $\Theta_\cA$ in a modular convolution
Lie algebra, and the volume's theorems --- adjunction and inversion,
quadratic recognition, centre complementarity, the
deformation--Hodge--graph traces, Hochschild concentration --- are
its projections. The Koszul dual reflects parameters
($k\mapsto-k-2h^\vee$, $c\mapsto26-c$), the conductor laws
$c+c^!=2\dim\fg,\ 26,\ 4N^3-2N-2$ organise the landscape into five
archetypes, and the scalar genus tower is
$F_g=\kappa\lambda_g^{\mathrm{FP}}$, the $\widehat A$-genus in
disguise. This companion states the engine with its exact hypothesis
packages, the complete census of constants, the arithmetic shadow,
and the honest frontier.
\end{abstract}

\section{The seed}\label{sec:seed}

The product of two chiral fields $a(z)b(w)$ on a smooth algebraic
curve $X$ diverges along the diagonal. Among $1$-forms with a pole
there, exactly one has residue independent of every choice:
$\eta=d\log(z_1-z_2)$. Three-point consistency forces the Arnold
relation
$\eta_{12}\wedge\eta_{23}+\eta_{23}\wedge\eta_{31}
+\eta_{31}\wedge\eta_{12}=0$, which generates all relations in
$H^\bullet(\Conf_n(\bC))$; and $\eta$ fails coordinate-invariance by
the cocycle $d\log w'(z)$ --- the ghost the Virasoro algebra
measures. From this seed the whole volume unfolds. The ordered bar
complex of an augmented chiral algebra $\cA$,
\[
\barB^{\mathrm{ord}}_X(\cA)
=T^c(s^{-1}\bar\cA)
=\bigoplus_{n\ge1}(s^{-1}\bar\cA)^{\otimes n}\otimes
\Gamma\bigl(\FM^{\mathrm{ord}}_n(X),\Omega^{n-1}_{\log}\bigr),
\qquad
d=d_{\mathrm{int}}+d_{\mathrm{res}}+d_{\mathrm{dR}},
\]
extracts operator-product residues against the logarithmic kernel,
and $d^2=0$ holds \emph{if and only if} $\cA$ satisfies the Borcherds
identity: configuration-space geometry selects the chiral-algebra
axioms. At genus $g$ the difference $z_1-z_2$ has no meaning; the
prime form replaces it, the propagator acquires a period correction,
and the fibrewise differential is curved,
$d^2_{\mathrm{fib}}=\kappa(\cA)\cdot\omega_g$, flatness restored only
by the Gauss--Manin connection on the total space. The
genus-completed differential is strictly square-zero because the
Deligne--Mumford boundary satisfies $\partial^2=0$, and its positive
part
\[
\Theta_\cA:=D_\cA-d_0,
\qquad
D\Theta_\cA+\tfrac12[\Theta_\cA,\Theta_\cA]=0
\]
is a Maurer--Cartan element in the modular convolution dg Lie algebra
--- automatic, no equation to solve. Every invariant of the volume is
a projection of $\Theta_\cA$: the modular characteristic $\kappa$ at
degree two, the classical $r$-matrix as its binary collision residue,
the shadow tower beyond, the genus tower as its trace. The question
underneath, stated at the end of the volume: characterise the modular
convolution algebra as a geometric object --- proposed answer, the
endomorphisms of the Lagrangian embedding
$\cL_\cA\hookrightarrow\cM_{\mathrm{vac}}(\cA)$ in a $(-2)$-shifted
symplectic stack of vacua.

\section{The objects}\label{sec:objects}

\begin{definition}[Chiral algebra and its three bar complexes]
A chiral algebra on $X$ is a right $\mathcal D_X$-module with chiral
product $\mu\colon j_*j^*(\cA\boxtimes\cA)\to\Delta_!\cA$ satisfying
locality, the Borcherds identity, and unitality
\textup{(Beilinson--Drinfeld)}; augmented when
$\varepsilon\colon\cA\to\omega_X$ splits. Three bar complexes coexist
and are never conflated: the Francis--Gaitsgory bar \textup{(only the
top residue; the Lie shadow)}; the symmetric bar on $\Ran(X)$
\textup{(all operator products, $\Sigma_n$-coinvariants)}; the
ordered bar on ordered configurations \textup{(all products, no
quotient)}. The averaging map
$\av\colon\barB^{\mathrm{ord}}\to\barB^{\Sigma}$ is a surjective dg
Lie morphism whose kernel carries braid and associator data --- a
torsor over $\mathrm{GRT}_1(\bQ)$ at genus zero, the quasi-modular
anomaly of $E_2$ at genus one. What the symmetric theory forgets, the
ordered theory remembers, and the surplus is exactly the $R$-matrix.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The five typed objects]
For augmented $\cA$: the algebra $\cA$; the bar coalgebra
$B_X(\cA)$ \textup{(complete conilpotent; the twisting datum, never
the bulk)}; the quadratic coalgebra
$\cA^{\mathrm i}=C_X(s^{-1}V,s^{-2}R)$ of a chosen presentation; the
Koszul dual algebra $\cA^!$, reached by continuous Ran--Verdier
duality $K_X(\cA)=\Dver_{\Ran}B_X(\cA)$ --- holonomic
$\mathcal D$-module duality, never the linear dual; and the derived
chiral centre $\Zder(\cA)=C^\bullet_{\mathrm{ch}}(\cA,\cA)$, the
bulk. Bar--cobar inversion $\Omega_XB_X(\cA)\simeq\cA$ and Koszul
duality are different functors with different targets.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Modular characteristic]
$\kappa(\cA)$ is the scalar of the genus curvature:
$\operatorname{tr}_{\mathrm{diag}}(m_0^{(g)})=\kappa(\cA)\,\omega_g$
with $\omega_1=c_1(\bE)$ the first Hodge class --- equivalently the
cyclic trace of the self-sewn binary corolla, equivalently the
normalised residue trace of the collision kernel. From the ordered
side: $\kappa=\av(r_\cA(z))$ for abelian families, plus the Sugawara
self-contraction $\dim\fg/2$ for nonabelian Kac--Moody. Values:
$\kappa(\cH_k)=k$ \textup{(the level, not $c/2$)};
$\kappa(V_k(\fg))=\dim\fg\,(k+h^\vee)/2h^\vee$;
$\kappa(\Vir_c)=c/2$; $\kappa(\cW_N)=c\,(H_N-1)$ with $H_N$ the
harmonic number; $\kappa(bc_\lambda)=-(6\lambda^2-6\lambda+1)$,
$\kappa(\beta\gamma_\lambda)=+(6\lambda^2-6\lambda+1)$;
$\kappa(V_\Lambda)=\operatorname{rank}\Lambda$;
$\kappa(V^\natural)=12$.
\end{definition}

\section{The theorems}\label{sec:theorems}

\begin{theorem}[Selection principle; proved]\label{thm:selection}
For a meromorphic product datum on $X$, the Fulton--MacPherson bar
differential satisfies $d^2=0$ if and only if the product satisfies
the Borcherds identity: disjoint indices contribute Koszul signs,
triple collisions contribute the Arnold relation on forms against the
Borcherds identity on modes, and repeated pairs die because the
logarithmic normal factor is consumed by the first residue. The bar
complex is synonymous with the chiral-algebra axiom.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The adjunction and its honest scope]\label{thm:A}
The continuous cobar $\Omega_X$ is left adjoint to the bar:
$\operatorname{Hom}(\Omega_X\cC,\cA)\cong
\mathrm{Tw}(\cC,\cA)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\cC,\barB_X\cA)$ --- the
counit exists for every augmented chiral algebra, realised by the
residue pairing against logarithmic forms, with no Koszul hypothesis.
The enhanced statement --- mutually inverse bar and cobar on the
pro-nilpotent Francis--Gaitsgory Ran category, transported to the
Fulton--MacPherson chain models and closed under factorization ---
carries three named packages \textup{(factorization closure,
completed chain comparison, Verdier transport)} whose construction
for the standard atlas is the open work; the ambient factorization
category itself remains to be constructed in full. The honest form:
the adjunction backbone and the chain-level geometry are proved; the
$(\infty,2)$-enhancement is an architecture whose load rests on its
packages.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Koszulness; proved by the PBW criterion]\label{thm:B}
If $\cA$ carries a flat PBW filtration with classically Koszul
associated graded and finite-dimensional Mittag--Leffler windows,
then the twisted tensor product is acyclic and the quadratic
comparison $q_\cA\colon\cA^{\mathrm i}\to B_X(\cA)$ is a
quasi-isomorphism. Consequences: affine Kac--Moody $\widehat\fg_k$ is
chirally Koszul at generic level; the Virasoro algebra is chirally
Koszul for all $c$ \textup{(the quartic pole feeds curvature, not the
graded bracket)}. Seven diagnostics are equivalent on the recognition
locus, with the PBW $E_2$-collapse of the bar spectral sequence as
hub: Koszul twisting morphism; counit and unit equivalences; acyclic
twisted products; diagonal bar concentration; diagonal Ext vanishing
$\Ext^{p,q}(\mathbf 1,\mathbf 1)=0$, $p\neq q$; Shapovalov
non-degeneracy in the bar range. Koszulness is a moduli problem over
the associator torsor, and it is $\mathrm{GRT}_1$-invariant. Off the
locus: the critical-level algebra $V_{-h^\vee}(\fg)$ is not Koszul
\textup{(the Feigin--Frenkel centre spreads off-diagonal)}; the
$c_{3,4}$ minimal model is not \textup{(a null vector enters the bar
range)}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Curvature and the coderived ambient]\label{thm:curv}
Twisting the curved cobar by $\alpha$ has square
$[h_\Omega+d\alpha+\tfrac12[\alpha,\alpha],-]$; genus-zero
conilpotent objects live in the ordinary derived category, while
genus $\ge1$ with $\kappa\neq0$ is intrinsically coderived
\textup{(Positselski's second kind)}. The Heisenberg genus-one trace
cocycle $\omega_\ell(x_m,y_n)=\ell\,m\,(x,y)\,\delta_{m+n,0}$ is a
nonzero Chevalley--Eilenberg class, and the completed cobar of the
finite trace quotient recovers the centrally extended loop algebra:
this is the mechanism by which the Koszul dual acquires curvature.
The Feigin--Frenkel reflection $k\mapsto-k-2h^\vee$ is the unique
affine-linear involution fixing the critical level and compatible
with $\kappa$; class-$\mathsf M$ chain-level comparisons hold in the
pro, adic, or filtered-complete ambients and are provably false on
the bounded direct sum --- the harmonic discrepancy family
$\delta_r^{\mathrm{harm}}=c_r(\cA)\,m_0^{\lfloor r/2\rfloor-1}$
admits no single absorbing homotopy.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Complementarity; the scalar law]\label{thm:C}
The centre complex $\mathbf C_{g,n}(\cA)=
R\Gamma(\Mbar_{g,n},\cZ(\cA))$ splits by the represented Verdier
involution into complementary halves
$\mathbf Q_{g,n}(\cA)\oplus\mathbf Q_{g,n}(\cA^!)$; under the
perfectness package the halves are Lagrangian, dual up to the shift
$-(3g-3+n)$ --- what one algebra sees as deformation, its dual sees
as obstruction. The scalar shadow is the conductor law
\[
\kappa(\cA)+\kappa(\cA^!)=\varkappa:
\qquad
0\ \textup{(free fields, lattices, affine Kac--Moody)},\quad
13\ \textup{(Virasoro)},\quad
250/3\ (\cW_3),
\]
with $\varkappa=\varrho\cdot K$ on the $\cW$-lane, $\varrho(\fg)=\sum_i
1/(m_i+1)$ the anomaly ratio and $K=c+c^!$ the central-charge
conductor. The shifted-symplectic upgrade is a separate package.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The genus tower]\label{thm:D}
Four objects, four packages. The native class
$\Obs^{\mathrm{def}}_g\in H^2(\Def_g(\cA))$; its genus-one trace is
computed, $\operatorname{tr}_1\Obs^{\mathrm{def}}_{1,1}
=\kappa\lambda_1$; its $K$-theoretic avatar
$\fO^K_g=\kappa\,\lambda_{-1}(\bE_g)$ with Hodge character
$(-1)^g\kappa\lambda_g$ is a comparison hypothesis for $g\ge2$, not
a computation; Mumford's relation
$\Lambda_g(t)\Lambda_g(-t)=1$ gives $\lambda_g^2=0$, so the Hodge
shadow squares to zero wherever the comparison holds; and the
numerical trace is the Faber--Pandharipande integral
\[
F_g=\kappa\,\lambda^{\mathrm{FP}}_g+\delta F^{\mathrm{cross}}_g,
\qquad
\lambda^{\mathrm{FP}}_g=\int_{\Mbar_{g,1}}\psi_1^{2g-2}\lambda_g
=\frac{2^{2g-1}-1}{2^{2g-1}}\,\frac{|B_{2g}|}{(2g)!},
\qquad
\sum_{g\ge1}F^{\mathrm{sc}}_g x^{2g}
=\kappa\bigl(\widehat A(ix)-1\bigr),
\]
the cross term vanishing for free fields and equal to
$(c+204)/16c$ for $\cW_3$ at genus two. Clutching pins the class:
$\xi_h^*\lambda_g=\lambda_h\boxtimes\lambda_{g-h}$,
$\xi^*_{\mathrm{irr}}\lambda_{-1}(\bE_g)=0$, and a boundary-normalised
tautological class agrees with $\lambda_g$ on the socle quotient ---
on the nose at $g\le2$, $\lambda_g$-conjecture territory beyond. The
honest summary: genus one is a theorem, the higher-genus scalar lane
is classical Mumford--Faber--Pandharipande riding a named comparison
package, and the non-scalar tower is the open object.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Hochschild concentration]\label{thm:H}
The chiral Hochschild complex --- inputs and output on
$\overline C_{n+2}(X)$ against logarithmic forms --- computes the
derived centre, with the first-page spectral sequence
$H^q(\overline C_{n+2},\cA^{\boxtimes(n+2)}\otimes\Omega^n_{\log})
\Rightarrow\ChirHoch^{n+q}$. Given a family retract onto a model
supported in finite $S\subset\bZ$, the support is bounded by $S$; the
anchors are Bakalov--De~Sole--Kac: Virasoro support $\{0,2,3\}$ with
one dimension each, and at the critical level
$\ChirHoch^\bullet(\widehat\fg_{-h^\vee})\cong
\Lambda(P_1,\dots,P_r)\otimes\bC[\Theta_1,\dots,\Theta_r]$, degrees
tied to the exponents --- unbounded, the theorem's own boundary. The
force of the theorem for a new family is exactly the construction of
its retract; the confrontation with the nonvanishing variational
Poisson cohomology of De~Sole--Kac is the volume's sharpest open
front.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The ordered face]\label{thm:E1}
Descending the ordered bar local system along
$\Conf^{\mathrm{ord}}_n\to\Conf_n$ requires a $\Sigma_n$-equivariant
structure; the construction of that descent datum \emph{is} the
$R$-matrix, realised as monodromy of the Kohno connection whose
coefficients are bar collision residues. Consequences, each on its
stated locus: the ordered Koszul dual of the Heisenberg algebra is
the abelian Yangian $\bC[h_0,h_1,\dots]$; for the Yang kernel,
Verdier duality inverts the $R$-matrix, giving the self-duality
$Y_\hbar(\fsl_2)^!\cong Y_{-\hbar}(\fsl_2)$ with
$\hbar=1/(k+h^\vee)$, extended to all classical types on finite
windows and reduced, for exceptional types, to a named finite
package; genus-one monodromy exponentiates the additive parameter to
$q=e^{\pi i\hbar}$ --- a monodromy readout, deliberately not an
algebra identification; and Drinfeld--Kohno becomes the statement
that Koszul duality reverses collision cycles,
$\mathcal R\mapsto\mathcal R^{-1}$, $q\mapsto q^{-1}$, on the
integrable fusion surface. The Francis--Gaitsgory theory is the
commutator shadow: a spectral sequence from the Lie bar of the
associated graded converges to the graded ordered bar. At the
critical level the bar computes opers,
$H^n(\barB(\widehat\fg_{-h^\vee}))\cong
\Omega^n(\Op_{\fg^\vee}(D))$ --- degree zero is Feigin--Frenkel, the
higher degrees ride the Frenkel--Teleman comparison --- and the
reflection
$\kappa(V_k)+\kappa(V_{k^\vee})=0$ solves to
$k^\vee=-k-2h^\vee$ --- the Langlands-dual level as a curvature
equation.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[The averaging conductor; proved]\label{thm:av}
The ordered-to-symmetric averaging $\av$ is a natural dg Lie
morphism, simultaneously quotient, Reynolds fixed-point summand, and
homotopy-coinvariant shadow, lossy exactly on the nontrivial isotypic
sector with kernel dimension $d^n-\binom{n+d-1}{d-1}$ in arity $n$.
Its scalar avatars: $K^c=c+c^!$; $K^\kappa=\kappa+\kappa^!$; and the
ghost form $K=-c_{\mathrm{ghost}}(\mathrm{BRST})$, whence the
conceptual origin of the $\cW$-conductor:
$K_N=\sum_{j=2}^N2(6j^2-6j+1)=4N^3-2N-2$, one $bc$-ghost per spin.
\end{theorem}

\section{The garden}\label{sec:garden}

One logarithmic $1$-form generates the programme; this volume is
where the generation happens, so the map here is outward. Every
object sits on one chain: primitive factorization category $\to$
chart algebra $A_b$ $\to$ bar shadow $\barB(A_b)$ $\to$ derived
chiral centre $\Zder(A_b)$ \textup{(the bulk)} $\to$ acting line
operators $\to$ scalar modular shadow. Identifying stages without the
licensing datum --- shadow $=$ object --- is the master error the
programme is disciplined against.

\emph{Volume II} (\texttt{chiral-bar-cobar-vol2}) reads this volume's
engine as physics: bar $=$ BV/BRST, quantum master equation $=$
Maurer--Cartan, the anomaly identity
$Q^2_{\mathrm{BRST}}=\frac{c-26}{12}c_0\leftrightarrow
d^2_{\mathrm{fib}}=\kappa_{\mathrm{tot}}\omega_1$ with
$\kappa_{\mathrm{tot}}=(c-26)/2$, the two-coloured Swiss-cheese
operad as bulk--boundary home, and Universal Holography
$\cA\mapsto(\partial=\cA,\ \mathrm{bulk}=\Zder(\cA))$ --- at
$\Vir_{c=3\ell/2G_N}$ the boundary reading of 3d gravity, with the
same conductor $26$ appearing as the critical dimension.
\emph{Volume III} (\texttt{calabi-yau-quantum-groups}) constructs the
geometric source: the two-stage functor
$\Phi_d=\SpCh_{\Sigma_{d-1},C}\circ\Phi^{\mathrm{FA}}_d$ from
Calabi--Yau categories to chiral algebras, the cohomological Hall
positive half and its Drinfeld double as the level-three quantum
group, the $\kappa$-tuple $(0,0,3,5,24)$ at $K3\times E$.
\emph{Mixed holomorphic--topological strings} computes the local germ
of the two-dimensional theory at $N$ Dirac branes --- the trace
coordinate $J(f)=\Tr f(\phi_1,\phi_2)$ with Capelli anomaly --- and
the typed ledger separating germ from globe. \emph{The Igusa cusp
form} (\texttt{igusa-cusp-form}) is the terminal scalar: $\Dfive$
read five ways, $Z^{K3\times E}_{\mathrm{BPS}}=\Dfive^{-2}$, the
order-two orientation bit that squaring kills --- the sharpest single
example of this volume's slogan that the scalar shadow underdetermines
the object.

The single question underneath: characterise

\section{The frontier}\label{sec:frontier}

The honest list, in the volume's own post-audit order:

\begin{enumerate}
\item The ambient factorization category for the full
$(\infty,2)$-form of the adjunction --- until constructed, the
enhanced Theorem A reads: where such a category exists, bar--cobar
and Positselski apply in it.
\item The Hochschild engine: ordered twisted-tensor acyclicity, and
the confrontation with De~Sole--Kac nonvanishing --- the single most
important unresolved mathematical question of the volume.
\item The genus tower beyond the scalar-diagonal lane: the
identification $\Obs^{\mathrm{def}}_g=\kappa\lambda_{-1}(\bE_g)$ for
$g\ge2$ as computation rather than package; the non-scalar
Maurer--Cartan obstruction, governed by Bianchi, not killed by
Mumford.
\item Class $\mathsf M$ on the bounded direct sum --- provably false
there; the completed ambients are the theorem, the descent is the
question.
\item Fingerprint completeness: the five-slot invariant is a proved
projection; the reconstruction direction rides a package that
essentially assumes it.
\item The arithmetic superstructure: the uniform Kummer-absence
threshold; the noncommutative Weil dictionary
\textup{($\Theta_\cA\leftrightarrow$ Frobenius, positivity
$\Rightarrow$ Ramanujan)} --- a suggestive dictionary whose mechanism
is open, with Ramanujan for lattices imported from Deligne;
the $K3$ ladder of recognition targets \textup{(tree $\Dfive$,
one-loop Heegner, three-loop
$\zeta(3)$, $25/3$, $\Phiten^{\mathrm{un}}/\eta^{24}$)}, every rung
conjectural.
\item Bershadsky--Polyakov's genus-one curvature --- the one census
row still open.
\end{enumerate}

What stands without hypothesis: the selection principle; the
adjunction backbone; the ordered bar functor and its shuffle theorem;
the PBW recognition of Koszulness with its Kac--Moody and Virasoro
instances; the curvature mechanism and the central-extension
extraction; the completion theory; the averaging conductor; the
census with its three-way conductor derivations; genus one of the
tower; the Verlinde--Bernoulli arithmetic and the motivic purity of
the Virasoro shadow. The volume's deepest discipline is its own
maxim --- primitive objects first, shadows second, scalar modular
forms last --- and the theorems above are exactly the places where a
shadow was promoted to an object by an honest construction.
