\begin{abstract}
The two disjoint adjacent collisions of four ordered inputs define a square of coefficient forms.
Weighted integration on this square supplies the double-pair component of a native Heisenberg comparison.
Its boundary identity determines the remaining defects on the triple, double-pair, and small diagonals.
An explicit complex of translated current modules absorbs these defects at the original nonzero level.
The construction gives a permutation-equivariant cochain map for every global four-input coefficient.
The cohomology of the added complex and the resulting relative cohomology are described by Weyl-module presentations.
Ordered three-plus-one cuts agree with the ternary comparison on their factorization domains.
A torsion calculation obstructs the corresponding strict two-plus-two extension.
\end{abstract}
